# MS-PROT-043 — Public Enquiry Entry & Preparation Commercial Access Amendment

**Document ID:** MS-PROT-043
**Version:** 1.6
**Status:** ACCEPTED
**Approved:** 14 September 2026 — explicit manual approval of the complete proposal and limited formalisation scope
**Authority type:** Enquiry-owned supporting-access classification
**Governed by:** DESIGN-RULES v2.3; DOCUMENT-GOVERNANCE v2.3; MS-FUNDAMENTAL-VISION-001
**Amends:** MS-PROT-043 v1.4 §§2–7 within commercial classification of public entry and preparation
**Depends on:** MS-PROT-043 v1.2 §§24–28, v1.4 and v1.5; MS-PROT-046 v1.4; MS-PROT-049 v1.0 §12, v1.2 and v1.4; MS-PROT-050 v1.2 §§31–33; composite MS-PROT-062; MS-PROT-056 v1.7 §§5, 10 and v1.9 §§4–6
**Implementation activation:** NONE
**Purpose:** Classify the bounded Enquiry entry and preparation responsibilities without granting submission permission or introducing additional business prerequisites.

## 1. Governing decision

Enquiry SHALL define:

```text
enquiry/public-interaction-preparation-access@1
```

**Commercial classification:** No independent Commercial Entitlement required for the Enquiry-owned entry and non-committing preparation responsibilities defined below.

This classification SHALL NOT satisfy or replace `ORIGINATE_ENQUIRY`.

It creates no new public endpoint, form-builder language, persisted draft model or Enquiry lifecycle.

## 2. Scope

The contract covers the Enquiry-owned contribution and preparation associated with `enquiry/send-enquiry` for exactly:

- merchant-general Enquiry without a subject;
- Opportunity-linked Enquiry governed by `enquiry/opportunity-submission-access@1`.

Other subject-specific portfolios are not admitted by this contract.

Surface retains ownership of contribution composition and binding projection. This amendment classifies Enquiry’s participation in those processes; it does not create a general Surface entitlement policy.

## 3. Identity and ownership

The access contract is identified by its exact owner, identifier and revision. Its meaning SHALL remain immutable.

Its identity is not an entitlement identity, submitted Enquiry identity, Exposure contract or transport endpoint.

Enquiry owns its requirements and preparation. Source owners retain their data and participation rules. Commercial owns submission-grant evaluation.

Future contract revisions or additional subject families SHALL NOT inherit this exemption automatically.

## 4. Entry-point legitimacy

The absence of an independent preparation entitlement SHALL NOT manufacture an Enquiry interaction.

The entry point SHALL still derive from currently applicable, registered `enquiry/send-enquiry` semantics in the exact Merchant Scope.

For merchant-general Enquiry:

- no subject is required;
- absence of an Opportunity SHALL NOT prevent the general path;
- no Offering, Booking, Appointment, Order or Payment SHALL be fabricated to supply context.

This amendment adds no profile-completeness or business-category prerequisite.

It does not exempt the enclosing website or another delivery surface from its independently governed requirements.

## 5. Preparation contract

Preparation SHALL consume:

- exact trusted Merchant Scope;
- current legitimate Enquiry interaction context;
- an exact subject binding for Opportunity-linked preparation;
- applicable Enquiry-owned requirements;
- customer-supplied input;
- source information that the current context is authorised to use.

Preparation SHALL determine the information still required under MS-PROT-043 v1.2 §§24–28 and v1.4 §7.

Known authorised context SHALL be carried forward. Required unresolved information SHALL be requested. Irrelevant information SHALL NOT be introduced merely to fill a generic form.

Preparation SHALL NOT itself establish an accepted Enquiry, Conversation, CustomerContext, Attention occurrence, Notification responsibility or business commitment.

## 6. Subject and customer-data boundaries

Opportunity preparation SHALL preserve the exact semantic subject and validate its current participation under MS-PROT-043 v1.4 §§4–6 and MS-PROT-046 v1.4.

An invalid subject SHALL NOT be silently removed to produce general preparation.

Known customer information may participate only through its accepted relationship, access and data-use authority.

This exemption SHALL NOT grant:

- customer-record discovery;
- contact-value-based identity matching;
- unrestricted CustomerContext history;
- stored Enquiry observation;
- Conversation access;
- access to private subject information.

A source access requirement that remains unresolved SHALL NOT be treated as commercially or otherwise exempt by this contract.

## 7. Submission remains independently protected

Submission SHALL use the exact applicable access contract:

| Submitted context | Required contract and purpose |
|---|---|
| No subject | `enquiry/general-submission-access@1` with `ORIGINATE_ENQUIRY` |
| Exact Opportunity subject | `enquiry/opportunity-submission-access@1` with `ORIGINATE_ENQUIRY` |

Successful preparation SHALL NOT be retained or presented as durable submission authority.

At submission, Enquiry SHALL revalidate its current commercial and other execution requirements under MS-PROT-043 v1.5 and composite MS-PROT-062.

Missing binding information SHALL NOT satisfy origination permission.

Presentation SHALL NOT claim guaranteed acceptance merely because preparation succeeded.

## 8. Business Hours and receipt boundaries

MS-PROT-050 v1.2 §§31–33 remain governing:

- `OPEN`, `CLOSED` or `UNSPECIFIED` operating status SHALL NOT by itself disable an otherwise legitimate Enquiry interaction;
- a location’s status SHALL NOT be misrepresented as merchant-wide status;
- operating status SHALL NOT establish human availability or a response-time promise.

Preparation is not receipt.

This contract SHALL NOT produce an acknowledgement claiming an Enquiry was accepted before authoritative submission succeeds.

It introduces no response deadline, monitoring cadence or infrastructure-uptime guarantee.

## 9. Failure, repetition and protection

Preparation introduces no authoritative business mutation or new cross-capability transaction.

Repeated preparation may produce different unresolved requirements when authoritative context changes. An earlier result SHALL NOT override current requirements or source access.

Technical failure, missing requirements, invalid subject context and Resource Protection outcomes SHALL remain distinct. They SHALL NOT become fabricated submission success or an invented commercial denial.

Resource Protection Admission remains independently applicable. Commercial exemption SHALL NOT authorise unrestricted preparation workload or invent queued execution when protection defers a request.

Actual submission retains its existing logical-request idempotency and concurrency rules.

## 10. Supporting-service boundaries

This preparation responsibility supports the FREE Enquiry portfolio and SHALL NOT introduce a separate BUSINESS or GROWTH prerequisite.

It does not grant website delivery, domain service, Profile administration, Publication administration, AI assistance, Notification delivery or continuing messaging.

Ordinary deterministic preparation SHALL NOT require live AI availability.

An AI-produced suggestion SHALL NOT establish source identity, satisfy missing requirements without evidence, approve execution or create a commercial grant.

## 11. Catalogue and conformance consequences

Catalogue assembly SHALL record this exact supporting contract and its explicit commercial classification.

It SHALL NOT invent a separate preparation entitlement for the scope covered here.

All independently required submission, source-access and delivery bindings must still resolve.

Downstream implementation SHALL demonstrate:

- exact scope and contract selection;
- legitimate merchant-general entry without fabricated subjects;
- context carry-forward;
- protected-data access separation;
- no subject-dropping bypass;
- independent submission entitlement enforcement;
- no acceptance claim or business mutation during preparation;
- Business Hours independence;
- truthful failure and Resource Protection handling.

## 12. Closure and amendment effect

This amendment resolves only the commercial classification of the bounded public Enquiry entry and preparation responsibility.

It does not establish entitlement identities, publish a catalogue or resolve all dependencies of every Enquiry path.

`MS-PROT-056-V17-DQ-001` remains OPEN. Website delivery, remaining source-access classifications, exact grants and the complete manifest remain outstanding.

No implementation activation, C3 completion or programme promotion is authorised.

## 13. Review and falsification

**Vision result: VISION-CONFORMING WITH JUSTIFIED COMPLEXITY.** Customers answer only what remains unknown, while merchants avoid artificial transactional prerequisites. Internal access checks preserve privacy without introducing additional merchant configuration.

| Challenge | Required outcome |
|---|---|
| Information-only merchant has no Offering | General preparation remains possible |
| Merchant’s location is closed | Hours alone do not disable Enquiry |
| Opportunity is withdrawn during preparation | No silent conversion to general Enquiry |
| Customer enters an email matching another person | No identity association or private-data disclosure |
| Preparation succeeds but origination permission is absent | Submission remains denied |
| Preparation is retried after requirements change | Current requirements govern |
| AI is unavailable | Deterministic preparation remains independent |
| Website delivery permission is absent | Preparation exemption supplies no delivery grant |

**Alternatives rejected:** treating preparation as submission permission; charging separately for form preparation; requiring a transactional subject for general Enquiry; applying a blanket exemption to every supporting source.

**Ambiguity review:** preparation, accepted submission, source access, delivery and commercial permission remain distinct. No new data schema or generic availability model is inferred.

Evidence consists of accepted-authority inspection and these scenarios. Existing implementation was inspected as evidence, not treated as authority or proven conforming.
