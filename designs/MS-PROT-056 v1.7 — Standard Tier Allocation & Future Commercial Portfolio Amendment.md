# MS-PROT-056 — Standard Tier Allocation & Future Commercial Portfolio Amendment

**Document ID:** MS-PROT-056
**Version:** 1.7
**Status:** ACCEPTED
**Approved:** 14 September 2026 — explicit manual approval of the complete policy and limited formalisation scope
**Authority type:** Commercial allocation policy
**Governed by:** `DESIGN-RULES.md` v2.3; `DOCUMENT-GOVERNANCE.md` v2.3; MS-FUNDAMENTAL-VISION-001
**Amends:** MS-PROT-056 v1.1, within service-to-tier allocation scope only
**Preserves:** All other applicable MS-PROT-056 provisions through v1.6
**Implementation activation:** NONE
**Purpose:** Establish coherent commercial placement across FREE, BUSINESS and GROWTH, including financial operations, compensation, regulatory support and future commercial capabilities, without creating their underlying semantics.

## 1. Governing decision

MainStreet SHALL retain three standard subscription value levels:

| Tier | Governing value |
|---|---|
| **FREE — Establish** | Establish and maintain a useful professional public presence |
| **BUSINESS — Operate** | Perform the merchant’s supported day-to-day business operations coherently |
| **GROWTH — Grow** | Add governed marketing, analytical and advanced customer-engagement services |

Within a published standard catalogue revision:

```text
FREE grants ⊆ BUSINESS grants ⊆ GROWTH grants
```

A necessary part of an included BUSINESS workflow SHALL NOT be withheld solely to require GROWTH.

Commercial allocation SHALL NOT redefine capability ownership, business meaning, merchant policy, actor authority, provider readiness or execution approval.

## 2. Scope

This amendment determines **where service portfolios belong commercially**.

It does not define or publish executable entitlement grants.

Two allocation classes apply:

- **Defined-scope allocation:** commercial placement of an identifiable, already-governed semantic portfolio.
- **Future allocation reservation:** intended commercial placement whose exact service portfolio or required authority remains incomplete.

Neither class establishes production availability.

A reservation SHALL NOT be represented to merchants as a currently available subscription benefit.

## 3. Explicit non-goals

This amendment does not establish:

- prices, discounts beyond existing accepted policy, quotas or “unlimited” allowances;
- operational-location charges, negotiated capacity or support commitments;
- a new subscription lifecycle;
- production plan-revision identities, publication operations or effective dates;
- new capability operations, regulatory rules or analytical methods;
- native Invoice or Quotation authority;
- an unrestricted agentic CRM capability;
- provider selection, filing authority, payment authority or external communication permission;
- implementation readiness or completion of `IMP-08C-C3`.

Existing trial, agreement, Standing Free and residual-access semantics remain unchanged.

## 4. Ownership and admission

Commercial owns tier allocation, entitlement definitions, plan revisions and grant provenance.

Each referenced capability retains ownership of its facts, operations, invariants, failures and consequences.

Before an allocation becomes an executable grant, the catalogue design SHALL identify:

1. the exact accepted target and protected commercial purpose;
2. the stable entitlement definition identity;
3. applicable owner-qualified contracts;
4. prerequisite and supporting services;
5. the plan revision’s explicit grant set;
6. lifecycle, publication and historical-affinity rules;
7. required implementation and verification gates.

A feature name, PRD example, tier label or reservation SHALL NOT substitute for those definitions.

No runtime branch may infer a grant from the words “Business,” “Growth,” “AI,” “financial” or “advanced.”

## 5. FREE allocation

FREE SHALL commercially cover the following defined service purposes when their exact catalogue bindings are admitted:

| Portfolio | Boundary |
|---|---|
| Merchant public presence | Legitimately public business information, locations, hours and contact details |
| Merchant website delivery | Customer-facing presentation of authorised merchant material |
| Platform-provided website address | Platform-delegated service; no merchant-owned-domain claim |
| Presence and website assistance | Guided setup and content preparation, preserving merchant approval and source ownership |
| Product and service presentation | Either or both, according to the merchant’s applicable business model; not transaction execution |
| Publication | Accepted PublishedContent, Opportunity and Announcement operations and representations |
| Enquiry | General and explicitly registered subject-specific submissions, with authorised merchant observation |

FREE SHALL NOT require an information-only merchant to fabricate an Offering, Booking, Order or Payment merely to publish information or receive a question.

The following remain **FREE reservations** until exact service portfolios are defined:

- baseline discoverability services;
- presence-performance analytics.

Those reservations do not create visitor-counting, tracking, attribution or analytical authority.

## 6. BUSINESS allocation

BUSINESS SHALL include FREE allocations and the following defined-scope portfolios:

| Portfolio | Governing boundary |
|---|---|
| Booking, Appointment and Scheduling | Accepted customer-commitment and scheduling contracts |
| Orders, Payment participation and Inventory | Independently owned transaction, payment and stock semantics |
| Workforce Scheduling, Timekeeping, Leave and Rota | Composite MS-PROT-081 and MS-PROT-091 |
| Workforce Compensation and Payroll | Composite MS-PROT-080 |
| Financial Operations and Financial Evidence | MS-PROT-084 v1.1 |
| Initial Financial Health | The exact five families in Section 7 |
| Initial general operational BI | The exact seven families in Section 8 |
| Human customer communication | Accepted Conversation, Message, channel and access contracts |
| Routine factual customer-service responses | The initial response-contract portfolio in MS-PROT-086 v1.3 |
| Custom-domain website service | Commercial permission only; control evidence, routing and provider prerequisites remain independent |

BUSINESS also reserves commercial placement for:

- native invoicing;
- quotations;
- supported taxation calculation and filing services not yet admitted through exact regulatory and execution contracts.

These reservations SHALL NOT activate their missing semantics.

### Compensation boundary

Payroll treatment, calculations, document generation, delivery and worker access remain separately qualified.

A payslip SHALL NOT become an editable source of Payroll truth. Non-Payroll compensation documents SHALL NOT be universally labelled payslips.

### Regulatory boundary

Regulatory administration remains owned by MS-PROT-082 and the applicable accepted source/execution authorities.

This amendment selects no country, jurisdiction pack, tax rule, filing service or professional-support claim.

It does not rely on the unresolved lifecycle status of MS-PROT-082 v1.1.

## 7. Financial Health and monitoring

BUSINESS SHALL be the commercial placement for these five MS-PROT-084 v1.1 §22 families:

```text
financial-health/current-due-commitment-coverage@1
financial-health/near-term-commitment-pressure@1
financial-health/overdue-receivable-pressure@1
financial-health/financing-arrears@1
financial-health/regulatory-financial-pressure@1
```

Financial Health remains derived analytical meaning under MS-PROT-083. It does not become a second financial ledger.

Every assessment SHALL preserve its governed method, source coverage, evaluation time, currency qualification, lineage and unresolved conditions.

The assessment vocabulary remains:

```text
NO_MATERIAL_CONCERN
MONITOR
MATERIAL_CONCERN
UNKNOWN
NOT_APPLICABLE
```

Missing evidence SHALL NOT become zero or presumed financial safety.

### Monitoring allocation

Governed reassessment and handling of these indicators belong in BUSINESS.

This placement does not create execution mechanics:

- `MONITOR` SHALL NOT itself schedule a worker.
- Reassessment requires an accepted trigger or schedule contract.
- An assessment SHALL NOT automatically create Merchant Attention.
- Attention SHALL NOT authorise collection, payment, filing or another consequential operation.
- An unavailable monitoring path SHALL NOT be presented as active.

No universal financial score, profitability calculation, general ledger or unrestricted currency aggregation is admitted.

## 8. Operational BI

The following MS-PROT-083 v1.3 families belong in BUSINESS, using their existing `business-intelligence` namespace:

```text
order-commitment-count@1
appointment-occurrence-outcome-count@1
booking-utilisation-outcome-count@1
outstanding-payment-obligation-count@1
outstanding-payment-obligation-value@1
inventory-position-constraint-count@1
scheduled-commitment-person-duration@1
```

Their accepted definitions, source requirements and coverage qualifications remain unchanged.

Ownership by Business Intelligence SHALL NOT automatically imply GROWTH placement.

Access to authoritative operational records SHALL NOT be reclassified as advanced analytics merely because those records support an analytical calculation.

## 9. GROWTH allocation

GROWTH SHALL include BUSINESS allocations.

The accepted Campaign and audience portfolios governed by composite MS-PROT-087 belong in GROWTH, subject to exact catalogue admission.

The following are **GROWTH reservations**, not wildcard grants:

- additional qualified forecasting and scenario services;
- additional analytical optimisation services;
- agentic CRM.

### Agentic CRM reservation

“Agentic CRM” is a product-intent label, not an accepted semantic owner or executable operation.

A future portfolio must define its goals, permitted actions, evidence, approval boundaries, stopping conditions, retries and external effects through the normal design lifecycle.

The reservation SHALL NOT permit autonomous customer commitments, unapproved outbound communication, invented customer facts or cross-capability mutation.

Ordinary customer records, human communication and the initial bounded factual response portfolio remain BUSINESS scope.

## 10. Supporting services and platform quality

Supporting services SHALL be evaluated with the service they support, not automatically sold as separate architectural components.

In particular:

- Necessary attention and notification paths must not become a separate premium obstacle to completing an included workflow.
- Document intake and extraction under MS-PROT-092 remain evidence coordination, not invoice, tax, payroll or identity authority.
- AI-assisted interaction does not acquire broader permission than the underlying purpose.
- Ordinary deterministic operations and valid published websites must survive live AI unavailability.
- Security, tenant isolation, accessibility, correctness and data integrity SHALL NOT be premium tier differentiators.

This amendment introduces no new paid differentiation for the client and presentation architecture governed by MS-PROT-093 and MS-PROT-094.

## 11. Commercial lifecycle and historical preservation

Existing MS-PROT-056 rules remain controlling:

- Merchant configuration follows supported business needs independently of tier.
- Commercial permission does not activate capabilities or grant actor privileges.
- Standing Free, trial and paid-agreement grants remain independent sources.
- Published revisions retain explicit immutable grant meaning.
- Catalogue hierarchy is validated at publication, not through mutable live inheritance.
- Later packaging changes do not silently migrate Standing Free baselines.
- Entitlement loss does not delete business history or override accepted residual commitment access.

A future catalogue must reconcile these allocations into exact snapshots. This amendment does not perform that publication.

## 13. Fundamental Vision Conformance

**Outcome: VISION-CONFORMING WITH JUSTIFIED COMPLEXITY for this allocation-policy scope.**

The proposal supports:

- useful establishment without forcing transaction capabilities;
- coherent daily operation without artificial paid-tier fragmentation;
- financial and workforce understanding within BUSINESS;
- additional Growth services without making basic operation depend on them;
- business-language assistance rather than merchant administration of software internals.

Complexity remains in entitlement admission, historical provenance and bounded service coordination. That complexity is justified by correctness and does not require a merchant-facing entitlement editor.

Native capability ownership and provider infrastructure remain distinct.

## 14. Review and falsification

The evidence here is authority inspection and scenario analysis—not production testing.

| Challenge | Result |
|---|---|
| Information publisher has no sales workflow | FREE supports publication and enquiry without fabricated commerce |
| Salon needs scheduling, staff and payslips | Proposed BUSINESS placement remains coherent; jurisdiction prerequisites still apply |
| Retailer needs orders, inventory and overdue-payment understanding | Initial operational and financial portfolios do not require Growth |
| Merchant operates several locations | Scale alone does not change standard tier |
| Financial evidence is incomplete or mixed-use | Coverage qualification and `UNKNOWN` survive commercial entitlement |
| Two facts represent one economic exposure | Source relationship and non-duplication rules remain mandatory |
| Document extraction recognises an invoice | No native Invoice or payment authority is created |
| Customer requests a refund from a factual responder | Response permission does not authorise the refund |
| Indicator returns `MONITOR` | No unstated schedule, attention occurrence or action follows |
| Growth ends but Business remains | Proposed BUSINESS portfolios are not removed with Growth-only grants |
| AI or a delivery provider fails | Business truth and independent deterministic operations survive |
| Ordinary worker views compensation information | Relationship, authorisation and Exposure remain controlling |

These cases support the allocation boundaries. They do not establish arbitrary platform generality, operational performance or implementation completeness.

## 15. Alternatives and trade-offs

**Rejected: all finance or all BI in GROWTH.**
The accepted initial portfolios include ordinary operational and financial understanding.

**Rejected: all automation in GROWTH.**
Necessary reminders, bounded factual assistance and ordinary coordination are not synonymous with advanced optimisation.

**Rejected: feature names immediately create entitlements.**
This would bypass capability admission and exact contractual meaning.

**Chosen trade-off:** BUSINESS contains substantial operating value. GROWTH must be justified by separately admitted additional value, not missing workflow steps.

This policy makes no claim that pricing or operating-cost feasibility has already been proven.

## 16. Ambiguity and readiness result

For this policy scope:

- Owners and negative boundaries are explicit.
- Tier allocations are distinguished from grants, reservations and readiness.
- Financial monitoring is distinguished from assessment vocabulary and execution scheduling.
- Future names do not become semantic types.
- Existing lifecycle, concurrency, retry and historical rules remain with their owners.
- No new authoritative mutation operation is introduced.
- Deferred work cannot be used to implement a current grant.
- The disputed jurisdiction amendment is not a dependency of the allocation decision.
- Implementation proof is expressly downstream.

The policy can therefore be considered separately from the executable catalogue. **The executable catalogue and C3 activation remain not implementation-ready.**
