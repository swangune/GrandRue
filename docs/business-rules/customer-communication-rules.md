# Customer Communication Rules — Historical Draft Evidence

> **Document ID:** BR-CC-01
> **Module:** Business Rules
> **Status:** Historical Draft evidence — non-authoritative
> **Current governing authority:** MS-PROT-086 v1.0 and its accepted dependency composition

The former draft remains available through Git history as product evidence. Its broad principles are governed more precisely by accepted authority.

Within overlapping scope, MS-PROT-086 replaces the draft's confidence-based AI-first rule, automatic Conversation conclusions, contact-based continuity implication, complete-history claim and unqualified provider/email assumptions.

Current authority requires contract-governed Conversation creation/reuse, strong continuity evidence, exact participant and access binding, immutable idempotent Messages, separation of Message acceptance from delivery/read and business acknowledgement, source-owner operations for business consequences, contract-grounded automated informational responses, truthful durable human handoff and data-minimised recorded history.

No production channel, automated customer-service response, guest continuation or Message attachment is activated by this historical document. Those portfolios remain gated by `MS-PROT-086-DQ-001` through `MS-PROT-086-DQ-004`.
