# **40. Product Success Metrics (KPIs)**

## **40.1 Purpose**

The Product Success Metrics define how the success of Main Street will be measured after launch.

The objective is not simply to measure platform growth, but to determine whether Main Street is successfully helping local businesses establish, operate and grow their digital presence.

Every metric should support informed product decisions and continuous improvement.

---

# **40.2 Design Philosophy**

The Product Success Metrics are founded on one principle:

> **Merchant success is the platform's success.**

Main Street succeeds when merchants become more visible, attract more customers, operate more efficiently and choose to remain on the platform.

Platform growth should therefore be measured primarily through merchant outcomes rather than vanity metrics.

---

# **40.3 Objectives**

The success metrics shall:

* Measure merchant adoption.
* Measure customer engagement.
* Evaluate platform effectiveness.
* Assess business growth.
* Guide product improvements.
* Monitor operational health.
* Support strategic decision-making.

---

# **40.4 Merchant Acquisition Metrics**

These metrics measure how effectively Main Street attracts businesses.

Examples include:

* Total registered merchants.
* New merchant registrations.
* Verified businesses.
* Website publication rate.
* Merchant activation rate.
* Geographic distribution.
* Industry distribution.

Success is measured by the number of legitimate local businesses successfully establishing an online presence.

---

# **40.5 Merchant Activation Metrics**

Registration alone does not indicate success.

A merchant is considered **active** when they have completed the essential onboarding process and their website is publicly available.

Activation indicators include:

* Website published.
* Google Business Profile connected or verification initiated.
* Business profile completed.
* At least one product or service published.
* Brand assets uploaded.
* Contact information completed.

The objective is to minimise the time between registration and becoming publicly visible.

---

# **40.6 Merchant Retention Metrics**

Long-term platform value is reflected by merchant retention.

Key indicators include:

* Monthly active merchants.
* Annual retention rate.
* Subscription renewal rate.
* Upgrade rate from Free to paid tiers.
* Downgrade rate.
* Account closure rate.

A high retention rate indicates that merchants continue to derive value from the platform.

---

# **40.7 Customer Engagement Metrics**

These metrics measure how customers interact with merchant websites.

Examples include:

* Website visitors.
* Returning visitors.
* Customer registrations.
* Anonymous purchases.
* Announcement views.
* Contact enquiries.
* Booking requests.
* Orders placed.

These metrics help evaluate whether merchant websites effectively engage visitors.

---

# **40.8 Commerce Metrics**

The platform should monitor commercial activity.

Examples include:

* Orders processed.
* Bookings completed.
* Payment success rate.
* Average order value.
* Average booking value.
* Merchant revenue processed through the platform.

These indicators demonstrate the platform's effectiveness as a business operating environment.

---

# **40.9 Google Visibility Metrics**

Since increasing local visibility is a core objective, the platform should monitor indicators such as:

* Businesses with verified Google Business Profiles.
* Businesses appearing in local search.
* Customer visits originating from Google Search.
* Customer visits originating from Google Maps.
* Review request completion rates.

Improved visibility represents one of the platform's primary value propositions.

---

# **40.10 Website Performance Metrics**

The platform shall monitor:

* Website availability.
* Average page load time.
* Mobile responsiveness.
* Image optimisation effectiveness.
* Error rates.

Reliable performance contributes directly to merchant credibility and customer satisfaction.

---

# **40.11 AI Effectiveness Metrics**

Artificial Intelligence should be evaluated by its practical impact.

Examples include:

* AI-generated website adoption.
* AI content acceptance rate.
* AI announcement usage.
* AI marketing usage.
* Time saved through automation.
* Merchant satisfaction with AI features.

The objective is to measure usefulness rather than novelty.

---

# **40.12 Subscription Metrics**

Commercial sustainability shall be measured through:

* Free tier adoption.
* Paid subscription growth.
* Revenue by subscription tier.
* Average revenue per merchant.
* Upgrade conversion rate.
* Feature utilisation by tier.

The permanent Free tier serves as the primary merchant acquisition channel, while premium features encourage upgrades based on demonstrated value rather than time-limited trials.

---

# **40.13 Platform Quality Metrics**

Quality indicators include:

* Customer-reported issues.
* Merchant-reported issues.
* Platform uptime.
* Failed transactions.
* Failed bookings.
* Support resolution times.
* Critical incident frequency.

Maintaining a dependable platform is essential for merchant trust.

---

# **40.14 Merchant Satisfaction**

Merchant satisfaction should be measured through:

* Product feedback.
* Feature requests.
* Merchant surveys.
* Net Promoter Score (NPS).
* Subscription renewal behaviour.

Feedback should directly influence future product improvements.

---

# **40.15 Strategic Success Indicators**

Main Street can consider itself successful when:

* Local businesses choose the platform as their primary online presence.
* Merchants upgrade because they recognise additional value, not because the Free tier expires.
* Customers interact directly with merchant websites without needing to understand the underlying platform.
* Businesses become more discoverable through search engines and local search.
* Administrative effort is reduced through intelligent automation.
* Merchants spend more time serving customers and less time managing technology.

---

# **40.16 Product Success Statement**

The success of Main Street is measured not by the number of features delivered, but by the measurable improvement it brings to local businesses.

Every metric should reinforce the platform's mission of enabling legitimate local businesses to establish a professional digital presence, strengthen customer relationships and achieve sustainable growth through intelligent technology that remains accessible, reliable and easy to use.
