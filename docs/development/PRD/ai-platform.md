# **36. Artificial Intelligence Platform**

**Revision:** 13 September 2026  
**Status:** Current product intent  
**Design authority:** MS-PROT-038, composite MS-PROT-057 and MS-PROT-094

## **36.1 Purpose**

The Artificial Intelligence Platform defines how AI assists users across Main Street without becoming a competing business engine.

AI is not the product core. Its purpose is to reduce ambiguity, repetitive work and technical interaction cost while preserving Main Street's registered semantics, deterministic validation, authorisation, capability ownership and authoritative state.

---

## **36.2 Governing Principle**

> **AI simplifies Main Street; deterministic Main Street operates the business.**

```text
Merchant
   ↓
direct interaction OR AI-assisted interaction
   ↓
same Main Street application contract
   ↓
authorisation
   ↓
deterministic validation
   ↓
capability-owned execution
   ↓
authoritative state
```

AI may interpret, clarify, draft, explain, recommend and prepare bounded proposals. It does not create a second runtime.

---

## **36.3 Objectives**

The AI Platform shall:

- let merchants use ordinary business language;
- reduce onboarding and reconfiguration friction;
- assist with content and media;
- explain current business state and outcomes;
- identify useful gaps or opportunities;
- help merchants invoke supported operations;
- use purpose-built, minimum-necessary context; and
- preserve merchant control and deterministic application authority.

---

## **36.4 Hard AI Boundaries**

AI shall not become:

- semantic authority;
- merchant-intent authority;
- business-state authority;
- authorisation authority;
- deterministic validation authority;
- a mandatory dependency for ordinary supported operations;
- a universal cross-capability transaction owner; or
- a generator of arbitrary per-merchant executable application semantics.

Model output remains a proposal or generated artefact until accepted by the appropriate deterministic contract and, where required, the merchant.

---

## **36.5 Merchant Intent and Natural Language**

AI may interpret merchant language and identify candidate meanings.

Where a material business meaning is inferred rather than explicit, Main Street must clarify or confirm the merchant's intent before promoting the inference into a semantic candidate or change.

Explicit instructions should not require redundant confirmation.

Unsupported merchant intent must remain visible as a product/configuration gap rather than being disguised by plausible generated output.

---

## **36.6 Onboarding and Configuration Discovery**

AI may:

- interpret merchant descriptions;
- map evidence to registered candidate semantics;
- identify unresolved decisions;
- reduce unnecessary questions;
- explain the proposed operating model in business language; and
- prepare bounded configuration intent.

AI shall not invent capabilities or semantic relationships, derive executable behaviour solely from business category, activate configuration, or treat model confidence as merchant approval.

---

## **36.7 Reconfiguration Assistance**

For an existing merchant, AI should begin from current authoritative configuration and relevant current state.

It may help distinguish a one-off operational instruction from a durable change to how the business operates.

Any resulting change uses the same deterministic review, validation, authorisation and execution contracts as a manually initiated change.

---

## **36.8 Website and Presence Assistance**

AI may assist with public copy, FAQs, media, search metadata, merchant-specific website composition and contextual website-improvement suggestions.

For customer-facing websites, AI may reason about presentation hierarchy, grouping, prominence, media emphasis, responsive composition and customer-action prominence within MS-PROT-094 constraints.

AI must not classify a merchant into a finite template, theme, named style, domain layout or Presentation Profile family as a substitute for merchant-specific composition.

The website remains a presentation-and-interaction delivery surface and does not own authoritative business data.

AI website assistance may not create new business behaviour merely because that behaviour would improve the site. If the merchant expresses new operating intent, the request moves to the appropriate reconfiguration/capability process.

Website incompleteness remains non-blocking for unrelated business operations.

An already valid published website remains serviceable without live AI.

---

## **36.9 Customer Assistance**

Where enabled, AI may answer or assist with customer interactions using approved business information and supported application/projection contracts.

It must not fabricate merchant facts or replace an authoritative operation with model inference.

A request that could create or change a business commitment must flow through the applicable Main Street application contract.

---

## **36.10 Operational Assistance**

AI may help merchants interpret business state, prepare customer communication, identify attention items, explain outcomes and prepare bounded action proposals.

Cross-capability coordination remains governed by the accepted application/orchestration authorities. The AI assistant does not become the semantic owner of coordinated work.

---

## **36.11 Content and Media Generation**

AI may generate or transform non-authoritative content and media where supported.

Generated material must remain distinguishable from authoritative business facts and must satisfy the applicable merchant review/publication rules.

---

## **36.12 Context Architecture**

AI context should be purpose-built and minimum necessary.

Context may contain appropriately authorised merchant configuration, capability-owned projections, profile facts, relevant operational state, registered semantic options and bounded presentation inputs appropriate to the specific inference responsibility.

The application remains responsible for assembling and qualifying that context.

AI context is not itself a new source of truth.

---

## **36.13 Model and Agent Architecture**

Main Street shall not assume:

```text
one capability = one agent
one responsibility = one deployed model
one merchant = one generated application
```

A coherent merchant-facing assistant may route among bounded inference responsibilities. Deterministic routing is preferred when application context already identifies the responsibility.

Model/provider choice remains replaceable infrastructure.

---

## **36.14 Ordinary Operations Must Survive AI Failure**

Every ordinary supported merchant operation must retain a deterministic non-AI path.

An AI outage or provider failure must not deactivate the underlying business capability.

Optional intrinsically generative enhancements may be temporarily unavailable without making the business operation unavailable.

Published customer-facing websites must likewise remain renderable without live AI.

---

## **36.15 Governance and Evidence**

Material AI-assisted actions must remain traceable through the accepted AI, application and audit boundaries.

The platform should preserve bounded inference contracts, validated outputs, applicable merchant approval, purpose-qualified context and safe model/provider replacement.

Website composition candidates remain presentation candidates until they pass the applicable validation/publication path.

---

## **36.16 AI-Era Strategic Role**

Main Street assumes general AI will continue improving at website, content and code generation.

The platform should use those capabilities where useful without treating generation as the product moat.

AI becomes materially more useful when it operates over trusted merchant context containing current configuration, permissions, commitments, relationships, availability, history and integration state.

That context and its governed execution are durable Main Street assets. The model is a replaceable mechanism for helping users interact with them.

For customer-facing website composition, AI should expand merchant-specific presentation possibilities without creating hidden template catalogues or business authority.

---

## **36.17 Artificial Intelligence Platform Statement**

> **Artificial intelligence is Main Street's assistance and interpretation layer. It makes a capability-driven operating platform easier to use and may assist merchant-specific website composition, but merchant intent, registered semantics, deterministic application contracts and capability-owned authoritative state remain the foundation.**
