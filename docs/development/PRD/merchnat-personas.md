# **12. Merchant Personas**

## **12.1 Purpose**

This section defines the primary merchant personas that Main Street is designed to serve.

These personas represent archetypal users rather than specific individuals. They provide a shared understanding of merchant needs, behaviours and expectations, enabling product, design and engineering teams to make user-centred decisions.

Every major feature should solve a genuine problem experienced by one or more of these personas.

---

# **12.2 Primary Persona: The Independent Business Owner**

### Profile

The Independent Business Owner operates a single local business and is personally involved in its daily operations.

Examples include:

* Barber
* Hairdresser
* Florist
* Café owner
* Electrician
* Plumber
* Mechanic
* Photographer

### Characteristics

* Owns the business.
* Usually employs fewer than five staff.
* Has limited time.
* Has limited technical knowledge.
* Wants professional results quickly.
* Makes purchasing decisions personally.

### Goals

* Be found on Google.
* Look professional.
* Receive more customers.
* Save time.
* Spend less on technology.

### Frustrations

* Building websites.
* Managing multiple subscriptions.
* Understanding SEO.
* Updating information in several places.
* Paying agencies for simple changes.

### Success Definition

> "I can manage my business online from my phone in just a few minutes each day."

---

# **12.3 Persona: The Family Business**

### Profile

A family-owned business operated by multiple family members.

Examples include:

* Restaurant.
* Grocery shop.
* Pharmacy.
* Bakery.
* Boutique.

### Characteristics

* Several people manage the business.
* Multiple staff need access.
* Information changes regularly.
* Reputation is extremely important.

### Goals

* Maintain consistent information.
* Allow staff to update products.
* Publish announcements quickly.
* Handle customer enquiries efficiently.

### Frustrations

* Different people updating different systems.
* Outdated information online.
* Inconsistent branding.

### Success Definition

> "Everyone updates one system and everything else stays in sync."

---

# **12.4 Persona: The Professional Practice**

### Profile

A licensed professional providing appointment-based services.

Examples include:

* Dentist.
* Solicitor.
* Accountant.
* Physiotherapist.
* Architect.
* Surveyor.

### Characteristics

* Reputation driven.
* Appointment focused.
* Requires trust.
* Values professionalism.

### Goals

* Generate appointments.
* Present expertise.
* Maintain professional branding.
* Build online reputation.

### Success Definition

> "My website reflects the professionalism of my practice without requiring constant attention."

---

# **12.5 Persona: The Mobile Service Provider**

### Profile

Businesses that travel to customers rather than relying on walk-in trade.

Examples include:

* Electrician.
* Plumber.
* Mobile mechanic.
* Cleaner.
* Gardener.
* Locksmith.

### Characteristics

* Works on-site.
* Frequently away from a computer.
* Relies heavily on mobile devices.
* Needs rapid updates.

### Goals

* Receive enquiries.
* Display service areas.
* Publish availability.
* Respond quickly.

### Success Definition

> "I can run my digital business from my phone while working."

---

# **12.6 Persona: The Growing Local Business**

### Profile

A successful local business preparing for expansion.

Examples include:

* Multi-location salon.
* Restaurant chain.
* Retail group.
* Fitness studios.

### Characteristics

* Multiple employees.
* Increasing customer base.
* Higher operational complexity.
* Interested in automation.

### Goals

* Standardise digital presence.
* Maintain consistent branding.
* Reduce administration.
* Scale efficiently.

### Success Definition

> "The platform grows with my business without becoming more complicated."

---

# **12.7 Shared Characteristics**

Despite operating in different industries, most Main Street merchants share common traits.

They generally:

* Are experts in their trade.
* Value simplicity.
* Prefer mobile-first workflows.
* Have limited time.
* Want more customers.
* Appreciate automation.
* Need affordable technology.
* Prefer practical outcomes over technical features.

---

# **12.8 Design Implications**

Every merchant experience should assume:

* The merchant is busy.
* The merchant is using a smartphone.
* The merchant has only a few minutes available.
* The merchant is not interested in technical terminology.
* The merchant expects immediate results.

Therefore, product design should prioritise:

* Minimal steps.
* Large touch targets.
* Plain language.
* Intelligent defaults.
* Fast loading.
* Automatic saving.
* Contextual guidance.

---

# **12.9 Accessibility of Expertise**

Main Street should make enterprise-quality digital capabilities accessible to businesses regardless of technical ability.

Merchants should never feel disadvantaged because they cannot:

* Build websites.
* Understand SEO.
* Configure infrastructure.
* Operate marketing software.

The platform should provide these capabilities automatically.

---

# **12.10 Persona Summary**

Although merchants operate different businesses, they all seek the same outcome:

> **Spend less time managing technology and more time serving customers.**

Main Street exists to make this possible through intelligent automation and invisible digital infrastructure.
