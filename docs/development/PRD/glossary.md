# **44. Glossary, Definitions & Product Terminology**

## **44.1 Purpose**

This glossary establishes a common vocabulary for everyone involved in the design, development, deployment and operation of Main Street.

Using consistent terminology reduces ambiguity and ensures that merchants, designers, engineers, product managers and stakeholders interpret the product in the same way.

Unless otherwise stated, these definitions apply throughout the Product Requirements Document.

---

# **44.2 Core Platform Terms**

### Main Street

The software platform that provides intelligent digital infrastructure for legitimate local businesses.

Main Street is not visible as a consumer-facing marketplace or social network. It operates behind individual merchant websites.

---

### Merchant

A registered business owner or authorised business representative who uses Main Street to manage their business.

---

### Customer

An individual who purchases products, books services or interacts with a merchant through the merchant's website.

---

### Visitor

A person browsing a merchant's website who has not yet become a customer.

Visitors may browse anonymously.

---

### Registered Customer

A customer who has chosen to create an account with an individual merchant.

Registration belongs to the merchant rather than Main Street.

---

# **44.3 Website Terminology**

### Merchant Website

The public website automatically generated and managed by Main Street on behalf of a merchant.

Customers interact directly with this website.

---

### Homepage

The primary landing page of a merchant's website containing the business profile, products, services, announcements and other business information.

---

### Announcement

A short business update published by a merchant.

Announcements are intended for communicating:

* Promotions.
* Business updates.
* New arrivals.
* Holiday hours.
* Events.
* Important notices.

Announcements are one-way communications.

Visitors cannot reply or comment.

Announcements may automatically publish to connected social media platforms.

---

### Business Sections

Configurable homepage sections that determine which content appears on the merchant's website.

Examples include:

* About Us.
* Products.
* Services.
* Gallery.
* Announcements.
* Testimonials.
* Contact Information.
* Opening Hours.

---

# **44.4 Commerce Terminology**

### Product

A physical item offered for sale.

---

### Service

A time-based activity performed by the merchant.

---

### Hybrid Business

A business that offers both products and services from a single website.

Example:

A salon that sells hair products while offering appointments.

---

### Booking

A scheduled appointment between a customer and a merchant.

---

### Order

A customer purchase of one or more products.

---

### Interaction

Any meaningful engagement between a customer and a merchant.

Examples include:

* Orders.
* Bookings.
* Enquiries.
* Customer registrations.

---

# **44.5 Business Terminology**

### Google Business Profile

A business listing managed through Google's business services.

Main Street assists merchants with connecting or creating a profile but does not perform business verification.

---

### Merchant of Record

The merchant remains legally responsible for:

* Sales.
* Services.
* Payments.
* Taxes.
* Refunds.
* Customer disputes.

Main Street provides the technology infrastructure only.

---

### Physical Business

A legitimate business operating from a verifiable physical location.

Main Street is designed primarily for these businesses.

---

# **44.6 Subscription Terminology**

### Free Tier

The permanent entry-level subscription.

Includes limited functionality and advertising.

It is not a free trial.

---

### Paid Tier

A subscription that unlocks additional operational, marketing and AI capabilities.

Merchants upgrade based on business value rather than time restrictions.

---

# **44.7 Artificial Intelligence Terminology**

### AI Assistance

Artificial Intelligence used to reduce merchant workload through automation and content generation.

AI assists merchants but does not replace business decision-making.

---

### Intelligent Simplicity

The foundational design philosophy of Main Street.

Advanced technology operates behind the scenes while merchants experience intuitive workflows requiring minimal technical knowledge.

---

# **44.8 Platform Terminology**

### Local-First

The principle that every feature should strengthen relationships between local businesses and their surrounding communities.

---

### Digital Operating System

The unified platform through which merchants establish, manage and grow their digital business presence.

Unlike a traditional website builder, Main Street combines website management, commerce, customer engagement, automation and business operations within a single platform.

---

### Powered by Main Street

A small attribution displayed in the footer of merchant websites.

Its purpose is brand recognition and platform awareness.

It should remain discreet and should never distract from the merchant's brand.

This glossary establishes the standard terminology for Main Street.
