# **43. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope**

**Revision:** 4 September 2026  
**Status:** Current product intent

## **43.1 Purpose**

This section defines the scope of Main Street's first public release.

The MVP is not intended to deliver every capability in the long-term operating platform. It must deliver the smallest coherent product that proves Main Street can establish a merchant operating model, expose it professionally to customers and execute a useful set of day-to-day operations reliably.

---

## **43.2 MVP Philosophy**

The Main Street MVP is founded on one principle:

> **Deliver a complete first operating loop, not a complete feature catalogue.**

Every Version 1 capability should materially contribute to merchant activation, customer interaction or the operational proof of the platform architecture.

Capabilities that introduce disproportionate regulatory, semantic or implementation breadth should be deliberately deferred rather than weakening the first release.

---

## **43.3 MVP Objectives**

Version 1 shall enable a merchant to:

- establish a merchant account/profile and supported initial configuration;
- publish a professional customer-facing storefront/website;
- publish supported products, services or information;
- accept Enquiries;
- accept an Order or Booking where the applicable capability is enabled;
- receive supported customer Payments;
- communicate important customer-facing information;
- manage supported staff access where included by product tier/scope; and
- operate from one coherent merchant dashboard/surface.

If these objectives are achieved with the required semantic, security and reliability evidence, the MVP is functionally complete.

---

## **43.4 Core MVP Features**

### Merchant Onboarding

- account establishment;
- adaptive business/configuration discovery;
- applicable trust/verification steps;
- business identity/profile;
- bounded brand/presentation setup; and
- generated storefront preview/publication.

### Public Storefront / Website

- mobile-first responsive presentation;
- approved public business information;
- Business Hours/Location where applicable;
- Products/Services/Publications required by the selected MVP merchant slices;
- Enquiry/contact entry points;
- appropriate customer action entry points; and
- bounded Main Street attribution according to product policy.

The website is a delivery surface over authoritative Main Street state, not the MVP's business authority.

### Business Management

- merchant dashboard;
- Product/Service management for selected slices;
- customer/Enquiry management;
- Order management where selected;
- Booking/Appointment management where selected;
- bounded Staff management where selected; and
- merchant configuration/settings required by the chosen capabilities.

### Commerce / Scheduling

Where deliberately included in the MVP validation slices:

- Order commitment;
- Appointment/Booking;
- supported Payment execution;
- appropriate confirmations/Notifications; and
- selected fulfilment/inventory behaviour required by the slice.

### Customer Experience

Customers shall be able to use the merchant-facing surface without needing to understand Main Street.

Applicable interactions may include browsing public information, Enquiry, Booking, Ordering, guest interaction and customer relationship/history where supported.

### AI Assistance

Version 1 AI should focus on reducing merchant effort without becoming business authority.

Examples include:

- onboarding interpretation;
- content drafting/improvement;
- image/media assistance;
- FAQ/public-copy assistance; and
- bounded merchant-operation assistance through deterministic application contracts.

### Marketing / Presence

Version 1 may include business Announcements, bounded syndication, review-request communication and basic authorised communications where their dependencies are ready.

### Analytics

Only analytics whose metric semantics, lineage and source facts are explicitly governed should be implemented.

The presence of an old PRD metric list does not authorise implementation to invent cross-capability metric meaning.

## **43.5 Features Deferred Beyond MVP**

The following capabilities are intentionally excluded from Version 1 unless a later approved sequencing change moves one into scope:

- **Payroll and workforce compensation administration**;
- payroll tax/statutory calculation and filing;
- payroll payout execution;
- workforce timekeeping/rota semantics beyond what selected scheduling slices require;
- multi-location business administration;
- advanced inventory breadth beyond selected validation needs;
- professional accounting-provider integration and statutory/general-ledger accounting beyond the bounded Financial Operations semantics accepted by MS-PROT-084;
- public developer APIs;
- third-party application marketplace;
- advanced AI business coaching;
- predictive analytics;
- demand forecasting;
- loyalty programmes; and
- enterprise administration breadth.

Payroll is a **committed long-term product capability**, not a rejected feature. It is deferred because production payroll introduces sensitive employee data, jurisdiction-specific statutory rules, approval/reconciliation requirements and provider/funds-movement failure modes that should not delay the first public release.

---

## **43.6 Success Criteria for MVP**

The MVP shall be considered successful if selected merchants can:

- activate a valid Main Street operating configuration;
- publish a professional customer-facing presence without technical assistance;
- manage the selected Product/Service/Publication information easily;
- accept and manage the selected Booking/Order/Enquiry interactions;
- receive supported customer Payments where applicable;
- operate from a mobile-first merchant experience; and
- experience measurable reduction in digital/operational administration.

---

## **43.7 MVP Boundaries**

The MVP intentionally does **not** attempt to become:

- a marketplace;
- a social network;
- a content-management/page-design platform;
- an enterprise ERP;
- a payment processor;
- a payroll bureau;
- a universal HR system; or
- a bespoke AI-generated application per merchant.

Its purpose is to prove the smallest coherent version of Main Street's capability-driven local-business operating platform.

---

## **43.8 Future Evolution**

Version 1 establishes the foundations upon which capabilities such as deeper Inventory, Analytics, multi-location operation, workforce administration and Payroll can be added without migrating the merchant into another product.

Future expansion must preserve:

- capability ownership;
- merchant control;
- local-first philosophy;
- AI as assistance rather than authority;
- provider neutrality; and
- a coherent operational model across delivery surfaces.

---

## **43.9 Minimum Viable Product Statement**

Main Street's MVP is deliberately narrower than the long-term product.

> **Version 1 proves the operating core. Later capabilities—including Payroll—extend that core only after their own semantics, security boundaries and failure contracts are ready.**
