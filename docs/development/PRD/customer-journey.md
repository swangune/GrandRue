# **14. Customer Journey**

## **14.1 Purpose**

While Main Street is built for merchants, its success depends on delivering an exceptional experience for the merchant's customers.

This section defines the complete journey of a customer interacting with a Main Street-powered business.

Unlike the Merchant Journey, the Customer Journey is intentionally invisible.

Customers should never need to understand that Main Street exists.

To them, they are simply interacting with the merchant's official website.

---

# **14.2 Journey Principles**

Every customer interaction should follow these principles:

* Fast.
* Simple.
* Mobile-first.
* Accessible.
* Trustworthy.
* Professional.
* Frictionless.

The customer should never encounter unnecessary barriers before contacting, booking or purchasing from a business.

---

# **14.3 Customer Journey Overview**

A typical customer journey consists of the following stages:

1. Discover the business.
2. Visit the website.
3. Explore the business.
4. Build confidence.
5. Take action.
6. Receive confirmation.
7. Return in the future.

Each stage should naturally lead to the next with minimal effort.

---

# **Stage 1 – Discover the Business**

## Objective

Enable customers to find local businesses wherever they normally search.

Customers may discover a business through:

* Google Search.
* Google Maps.
* Shared announcement links.
* Facebook.
* Instagram.
* LinkedIn.
* X.
* Threads.
* Search engines.
* QR codes.
* Printed marketing materials.
* Word of mouth.

Main Street should optimise every website for discovery without requiring merchant intervention.

---

# **Stage 2 – Visit the Website**

## Objective

Deliver a professional first impression within seconds.

The homepage should immediately communicate:

* Business identity.
* Products or services.
* Opening hours.
* Location.
* Contact options.
* Current announcements.

Visitors should never wonder:

* Is this business genuine?
* Is this information current?
* How do I contact them?

The answers should be immediately visible.

---

# **Stage 3 – Explore the Business**

## Objective

Allow visitors to understand the business quickly.

Visitors may browse:

### Business Profile

* About the business.
* Contact information.
* Opening hours.
* Address.
* Maps.
* Staff.
* Policies.

### Products

* Images.
* Prices.
* Descriptions.
* Availability.

### Services

* Service descriptions.
* Prices.
* Duration.
* Availability.

### Announcements

Recent business updates.

Examples include:

* Promotions.
* Events.
* New products.
* Holiday hours.
* Community activities.

Announcements reassure visitors that the business is active and engaged.

---

# **Stage 4 – Build Confidence**

## Objective

Help visitors feel confident enough to become customers.

Confidence is built through:

* A professional website.
* Consistent branding.
* High-quality images.
* Accurate business information.
* Google Business Profile verification.
* Positive customer reviews.
* Clear policies.
* Fast loading pages.
* Secure HTTPS connections.

Trust should be established before asking visitors to take action.

---

# **Stage 5 – Take Action**

## Objective

Enable visitors to complete their intended task with minimal friction.

Possible actions include:

### Contact

* Call the business.
* Send an enquiry.
* Request information.

### Visit

* Get directions.
* View opening hours.

### Purchase

* Buy products.
* Arrange collection or delivery (where offered).

### Book

* Schedule appointments.
* Select staff members (where applicable).
* Receive confirmation.

The experience should minimise unnecessary form fields and clicks.

---

# **Stage 6 – Customer Identity**

Main Street supports two interaction models.

## Guest Customer

Visitors may continue without creating an account.

They should be able to:

* Browse.
* Contact the business.
* Place eligible orders.
* Make bookings.

Guest checkout should remain available wherever practical.

---

## Registered Customer

Visitors may optionally create an account **with the merchant**.

Registration is intended to improve convenience for returning customers.

Benefits may include:

* Faster checkout.
* Saved contact details.
* Booking history.
* Order history.
* Saved preferences.
* Easier repeat purchases.

The account belongs to the merchant, not Main Street.

---

# **Stage 7 – Receive Confirmation**

## Objective

Provide reassurance immediately after a successful interaction.

Customers should receive timely confirmations for:

* Orders.
* Bookings.
* Enquiries.

All communications should appear to come directly from the merchant.

Main Street operates entirely behind the scenes.

---

# **Stage 8 – Return**

## Objective

Encourage repeat business.

Customers should naturally return because:

* The website is easy to use.
* Information remains accurate.
* Announcements keep the business current.
* Previous interactions are remembered (for registered customers).
* Repeat purchasing is simple.

Main Street should strengthen long-term relationships between merchants and their customers.

---

# **14.4 Customer Expectations**

Customers increasingly expect business websites to provide:

* Mobile-friendly design.
* Fast loading.
* Accurate information.
* Professional presentation.
* Secure transactions.
* Clear navigation.
* Simple booking and ordering.

Main Street should ensure every merchant meets these expectations by default.

---

# **14.5 Invisible Infrastructure Principle**

Throughout the customer journey:

* The merchant remains the visible brand.
* Main Street remains invisible.

Customers should perceive:

* The website as the merchant's website.
* Emails as the merchant's emails.
* Confirmations as the merchant's confirmations.
* Customer accounts as belonging to the merchant.

The platform should never compete with the merchant for customer attention.

---

# **14.6 Journey Success Criteria**

A successful customer journey is one in which a visitor can:

1. Find the business.
2. Trust the business.
3. Obtain the required information.
4. Complete an action.
5. Return again.

The process should feel intuitive and require minimal effort.

---

# **14.7 Customer Journey Statement**

The Customer Journey reflects Main Street's core philosophy of invisible infrastructure.

Customers should never think about the technology powering the experience.

Instead, they should simply experience a fast, trustworthy and professional website that enables them to interact confidently with the business.

When customers leave with a positive impression of the merchant, Main Street has fulfilled its purpose.
