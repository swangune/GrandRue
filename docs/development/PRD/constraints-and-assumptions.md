# **41. Product Constraints, Assumptions & Guiding Principles**

## **41.1 Purpose**

This section documents the fundamental assumptions, operational constraints and product principles that govern the design and evolution of Main Street.

These are intentional product decisions rather than technical limitations. They ensure that every future feature remains aligned with the platform's mission and identity.

Whenever a new feature is proposed, it should be evaluated against these principles before development begins.

---

# **41.2 Core Product Philosophy**

Main Street exists to remove digital barriers for legitimate local businesses.

The platform is designed around one central belief:

> **Running a business is difficult enough. Running its digital presence should not be.**

Every product decision should reduce complexity rather than increase it.

---

# **41.3 Intelligent Simplicity**

Main Street is built on the principle of **Intelligent Simplicity**.

Behind every merchant-facing feature is sophisticated technology, including AI, automation, cloud infrastructure and third-party integrations.

However, merchants should experience these capabilities through intuitive workflows rather than technical configuration.

Technology should disappear into the background.

---

# **41.4 Local-First Principle**

Main Street exists to strengthen local commerce.

Every feature should encourage stronger relationships between local businesses and their surrounding communities.

The platform is not designed to become a global marketplace.

Instead, it enables independent local businesses to compete effectively in the digital economy.

---

# **41.5 Merchant Ownership**

Merchants retain ownership of:

* Their business.
* Their brand.
* Their customers.
* Their products.
* Their services.
* Their pricing.
* Their policies.
* Their business decisions.

Main Street provides the digital infrastructure.

It does not become the operator of the merchant's business.

---

# **41.6 Main Street Is Not a Marketplace**

Main Street does not aggregate merchants into a shared shopping marketplace.

Customers visit individual merchant websites.

Each merchant maintains:

* Independent branding.
* Independent policies.
* Independent pricing.
* Independent customer relationships.

The platform should remain largely invisible to customers.

---

# **41.7 Main Street Is Not Social Media**

Although merchant websites contain an **Announcements** feed, Main Street is not a social networking platform.

The Announcements feature exists solely to help businesses communicate updates with customers.

Accordingly:

* Only merchants can publish announcements.
* Visitors cannot create posts.
* Visitors cannot comment.
* Visitors cannot follow businesses on Main Street.
* Visitors may share announcements externally using supported sharing mechanisms.

Announcements should also be eligible for automatic publication to connected social media accounts.

---

# **41.8 The Merchant Website Is the Product**

From the customer's perspective, Main Street should be invisible.

Customers interact directly with:

* the merchant,
* the merchant's website,
* the merchant's products,
* the merchant's services,
* the merchant's announcements.

The only visible reference to Main Street should be a discreet **"Powered by Main Street"** attribution in the website footer.

---

# **41.9 Permanent Free Tier**

The Free subscription tier is a permanent offering.

It is not:

* a trial,
* a limited-time promotion,
* or a temporary evaluation period.

Merchants should be encouraged to upgrade because additional capabilities provide greater business value—not because access expires.

The Free tier supports sustainable merchant acquisition while generating platform revenue through carefully integrated advertising.

---

# **41.10 Physical Business Requirement**

Main Street is designed exclusively for legitimate businesses with a verifiable physical presence.

Eligible businesses include:

* Shops.
* Restaurants.
* Salons.
* Tradespeople.
* Professional offices.
* Clinics.
* Studios.
* Local service providers.

Businesses without a genuine physical presence may not be supported where this conflicts with the platform's local-first mission.

---

# **41.11 Google Business Profile Principle**

Google remains responsible for verifying business legitimacy.

Main Street shall:

* help merchants connect existing Google Business Profiles,
* assist merchants in creating new profiles,
* monitor verification progress where technically possible.

Main Street does not replace or bypass Google's independent verification process.

---

# **41.12 Merchant Responsibility**

Merchants remain responsible for:

* Business operations.
* Customer service.
* Product quality.
* Service delivery.
* Legal compliance.
* Tax obligations.
* Business policies.
* Pricing decisions.

Main Street provides technology, not business management.

---

# **41.13 Customer Choice**

Customers should always have flexibility when interacting with merchant websites.

Where appropriate, they may:

* Register with the merchant for future convenience.
* Continue as returning customers.
* Proceed as guest customers without creating an account.

The platform should never require unnecessary customer registration.

---

# **41.14 Automation with Oversight**

Automation is encouraged throughout the platform.

However:

* AI should assist rather than replace merchants.
* Merchants should retain control over important business decisions.
* Automated actions should remain transparent.
* Human oversight should always be available where appropriate.

---

# **41.15 Future Decision Framework**

Every future product proposal should satisfy the following questions:

* Does this solve a genuine merchant problem?
* Does it strengthen local businesses?
* Does it preserve Intelligent Simplicity?
* Does it reduce manual work?
* Does it integrate naturally with the existing platform?
* Will merchants understand its value immediately?

If the answer to these questions is negative, the feature should be reconsidered before development.

---

# **41.16 Product Constraints & Guiding Principles Statement**

These constraints and guiding principles define the identity of Main Street.

They establish the boundaries within which the platform evolves, ensuring that future development remains focused on empowering legitimate local businesses rather than expanding into unrelated markets or unnecessary complexity.

By consistently applying these principles, Main Street can continue to innovate while remaining true to its mission: providing intelligent digital infrastructure that enables local businesses to thrive online without sacrificing ownership, independence or simplicity.
