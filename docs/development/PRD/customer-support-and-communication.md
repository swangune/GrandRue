# 29. Customer Support & Communication — Historical PRD Evidence

> **Status:** Historical product-requirement evidence — non-authoritative within accepted overlapping scope
> **Current governing authority:** composite MS-PROT-043, MS-PROT-059, composite MS-PROT-075, MS-PROT-085 and MS-PROT-086

This PRD records early product intent for simple, merchant-centred customer communication. Git history preserves its original detail.

Accepted authority now governs the overlapping scope with stricter boundaries:

- Enquiry, Conversation, Message, Notification, Merchant Attention and source business truth remain distinct.
- Conversation creation/reuse is contract-governed and requires strong continuity evidence.
- Provider threads and contact details do not define identity or grant access.
- Messages are immutable and idempotent; acceptance is distinct from delivery, read, understanding and business acknowledgement.
- AI may assist only under registered semantics. Confidence is not response or execution authority.
- Automated customer-service responses require an accepted response contract, current authorised evidence, sufficient coverage and deterministic validation.
- Human handoff may be claimed only after durable accepted handling evidence exists.
- Recorded communication history is data-minimised and must not be represented as a universally complete customer timeline.
- Internal staff notes require a separate typed protected model.
- Customer Communication remains below CRM, ticketing, workflow and enterprise contact-centre scope.

The PRD's channel examples, branded addresses, unified-inbox presentation, automated-response families, complete-timeline claim and future capabilities do not activate production behaviour.

Production channel, automated-response, guest-access and attachment selection remain gated by `MS-PROT-086-DQ-001` through `MS-PROT-086-DQ-004`. Concrete retention periods remain under `MS-PROT-043-V14-DQ-007`. Implementation and presentation require separate programme authority.
