# **13. Merchant Journey**

## **13.1 Purpose**

This section describes the complete lifecycle of a merchant using Main Street, from discovering the platform to operating and growing a successful business online.

The Merchant Journey is intentionally designed to minimise technical effort and maximise business value. At every stage, Main Street should automate complexity wherever possible.

The guiding principle is:

> **Every step should move the merchant closer to serving customers—not configuring software.**

---

# **13.2 Journey Overview**

The merchant lifecycle consists of nine stages:

1. Discover Main Street
2. Register
3. Verify Business Identity
4. Build the Business Profile
5. Publish the Digital Presence
6. Connect Business Services
7. Operate the Business
8. Grow the Business
9. Scale the Business

Each stage should feel like a natural progression rather than a technical setup process.

---

# **Stage 1 – Discover Main Street**

## Objective

Introduce Main Street as the simplest way for local businesses to establish a professional digital presence.

### Merchant Motivation

The merchant wants to:

* Get more customers.
* Be found online.
* Replace an outdated website.
* Start accepting bookings or orders.
* Save time.
* Modernise their business.

### Merchant Questions

Typical questions include:

* Can I build a website?
* Will customers find me on Google?
* Is it easy to use?
* Do I need technical skills?
* How much does it cost?

### Main Street Response

The platform communicates a simple promise:

> **Create your complete digital presence in minutes—not weeks.**

---

# **Stage 2 – Register**

## Objective

Allow merchants to create an account quickly with minimal friction.

Registration should request only essential information.

Examples include:

* Name
* Email address
* Password

Business details are collected afterwards.

The merchant should reach the dashboard within minutes.

---

# **Stage 3 – Verify Business Identity**

## Objective

Establish trust before publishing the business online.

Main Street should verify that the business is genuine while making the process as simple as possible.

### Existing Google Business Profile

If the merchant already has a verified Google Business Profile:

* Main Street requests permission to connect.
* Business information is imported where appropriate.
* Verification status is confirmed.
* Onboarding continues.

---

### No Existing Google Business Profile

If the merchant does not yet have a Google Business Profile:

Main Street provides a guided setup experience.

The platform:

* Explains why Google verification is important.
* Helps prepare the required information.
* Initiates the creation process where supported.
* Guides the merchant through Google's verification steps.

The merchant always understands what is happening and why.

Main Street assists, but Google remains responsible for verification.

---

### Verification Status

Possible states include:

* Not Started
* In Progress
* Pending Google Verification
* Verified
* Verification Failed

The merchant should always see the current status and any required next steps.

---

# **Stage 4 – Build the Business Profile**

## Objective

Transform business information into a professional digital presence.

Rather than building webpages, merchants complete a structured business profile.

Typical information includes:

### Identity

* Business name
* Logo
* Brand colour
* Cover image
* Theme (Light/Dark)
* Preferred font
* Business description

### Contact Information

* Phone number
* Email address
* Website domain (optional)
* Physical address

### Business Information

* Opening hours
* Business category
* Products
* Services
* Staff
* Service areas (where applicable)

### Customer Experience

* Booking preferences
* Payment options
* Delivery or collection options
* Business policies

As information is entered, Main Street automatically builds the website.

The merchant never edits pages manually.

---

# **Stage 5 – Publish the Digital Presence**

## Objective

Launch the merchant's website with a single action.

Publishing should automatically:

* Generate the website.
* Optimise images.
* Configure mobile layouts.
* Generate SEO metadata.
* Create structured data.
* Activate SSL.
* Publish announcements.
* Prepare search engine indexing.

The merchant simply selects:

> **Publish**

The platform performs the technical work.

---

# **Stage 6 – Connect Business Services**

## Objective

Allow merchants to extend their digital presence through optional integrations.

Examples include:

### Payments

* Connect Stripe.
* Enable online payments.

### Custom Domain

* Connect an existing domain.
* Register a new domain.
* Use a Main Street subdomain.

### Social Media

Connect business accounts such as:

* Facebook
* Instagram
* LinkedIn
* X
* Threads

Announcements can then be automatically syndicated.

### Email

Configure a business email address for customer communications where applicable.

Every integration should use guided workflows that minimise technical knowledge.

---

# **Stage 7 – Operate the Business**

## Objective

Support daily business operations through a simple mobile-first interface.

Typical daily activities include:

* Publishing announcements.
* Adding products.
* Updating services.
* Managing bookings.
* Managing orders.
* Updating opening hours.
* Responding to enquiries.
* Viewing analytics.

The merchant should complete most tasks within a few minutes.

---

# **Stage 8 – Grow the Business**

## Objective

Help merchants attract more customers without requiring marketing expertise.

Main Street should proactively recommend improvements.

Examples include:

* Completing missing profile information.
* Publishing announcements.
* Requesting reviews.
* Updating business photos.
* Running promotional campaigns.
* Improving local visibility.

Growth recommendations should be practical, actionable and prioritised.

---

# **Stage 9 – Scale the Business**

## Objective

Support merchants as their businesses become more successful.

Growth should not require changing platforms.

Capabilities may include:

* Additional staff.
* Multiple locations.
* Expanded product catalogues.
* Increased booking capacity.
* Advanced analytics.
* AI marketing.
* Higher automation limits.
* Premium infrastructure.

The experience should remain as simple for a larger business as it was for a single-location business.

---

# **13.3 Merchant Success Journey**

A successful merchant journey progresses through the following outcomes:

**Discover → Register → Verify → Publish → Operate → Grow → Scale**

At every stage, Main Street should remove unnecessary work while increasing the merchant's confidence.

---

# **13.4 Journey Principles**

The Merchant Journey should always satisfy the following principles:

* Minimal data entry.
* Mobile-first.
* Fast onboarding.
* Guided experiences.
* Intelligent defaults.
* Automation before configuration.
* Progressive disclosure of advanced features.
* No unnecessary technical language.
* Continuous value from the first day.

---

# **13.5 Merchant Journey Statement**

Main Street transforms the traditionally complex process of building and managing a digital business into a simple operational workflow.

Merchants should never feel as though they are configuring software.

Instead, they should feel as though they are simply managing their business while Main Street quietly handles the technology in the background.
