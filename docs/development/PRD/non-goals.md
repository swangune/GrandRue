# **10. Non-Goals**

## **10.1 Purpose**

A successful product is defined not only by what it does, but also by what it deliberately chooses **not** to do.

This section establishes the strategic boundaries of Main Street.

These non-goals are intended to:

* Prevent product dilution.
* Reduce unnecessary complexity.
* Maintain strategic focus.
* Guide product prioritisation.
* Prevent feature creep.
* Ensure long-term alignment with the platform vision.

Every proposed feature should be evaluated against these non-goals before being approved.

---

# **10.2 Product Scope**

Main Street exists to become the Digital Presence Operating System for local businesses.

The platform is **not** intended to solve every business problem.

Instead, it focuses exclusively on helping businesses establish, manage and grow their digital presence.

Capabilities that fall outside this objective should only be considered if they directly strengthen the platform's core mission.

---

# **10.3 Main Street Will Not Become a Website Builder**

Main Street deliberately rejects the traditional website builder model.

Merchants will not be expected to:

* Build pages.
* Drag and drop components.
* Edit HTML or CSS.
* Select templates from large galleries.
* Configure layouts.
* Manage responsive behaviour.
* Optimise websites manually.

The platform automatically generates professional websites from structured business data.

The business profile—not the webpage—is the product.

---

# **10.4 Main Street Will Not Become a Marketplace**

Main Street does not aggregate merchants into a marketplace where customers primarily purchase through the platform.

Customers interact directly with individual businesses.

Main Street facilitates discovery and digital engagement but does not seek to become an intermediary between merchants and customers.

Merchants own the customer relationship.

---

# **10.5 Main Street Will Not Become the Merchant of Record**

Main Street will not assume legal responsibility for commercial transactions.

The platform will not:

* Collect payments on behalf of merchants.
* Determine pricing.
* Handle taxation.
* Resolve customer disputes.
* Manage refunds.
* Fulfil products or services.

Merchants remain fully responsible for operating their businesses.

Main Street provides the digital infrastructure.

---

# **10.6 Main Street Will Not Operate Delivery or Logistics Services**

The platform will not build or operate:

* Delivery fleets.
* Courier services.
* Logistics networks.
* Warehousing.
* Inventory fulfilment operations.

Where appropriate, merchants may integrate approved third-party providers.

Main Street remains infrastructure rather than an operational service provider.

---

# **10.7 Main Street Will Not Become a Social Network**

Although the platform encourages customer engagement, it is not intended to become a social media platform.

The platform will not introduce:

* Public timelines.
* Social feeds.
* User-generated communities.
* Influencer ecosystems.
* General-purpose messaging networks.

Customer interaction should remain focused on legitimate business activities.

---

# **10.8 Main Street Will Not Become a General Advertising Platform**

The platform's purpose is to improve organic digital presence rather than become an advertising network.

Advertising should never compromise:

* User experience.
* Merchant trust.
* Platform performance.
* Search visibility.

Any future advertising initiatives must align with the platform's local-first philosophy.

---

# **10.9 Main Street Will Not Replace Professional Expertise**

Artificial Intelligence should enhance business operations rather than replace professional judgement.

The platform will not claim to replace:

* Lawyers.
* Accountants.
* Financial advisers.
* Medical professionals.
* Architects.
* Engineers.
* Licensed consultants.

AI-generated content should support merchant decision-making and remain subject to merchant review where appropriate.

---

# **10.10 Main Street Will Not Encourage Technical Complexity**

The platform will not expose advanced configuration unless there is a compelling business justification.

Features that require merchants to understand technical concepts should generally be avoided.

Examples include:

* Manual SEO configuration.
* Server administration.
* Performance tuning.
* Database management.
* Infrastructure management.
* API development.

Technical complexity belongs within the platform, not the merchant experience.

---

# **10.11 Main Street Will Not Compromise Performance for Features**

Feature growth must never come at the expense of performance.

The platform will not introduce capabilities that significantly degrade:

* Page speed.
* Mobile responsiveness.
* Reliability.
* Accessibility.
* User experience.

Performance is a product requirement, not an optimisation exercise.

---

# **10.12 Main Street Will Not Sacrifice Simplicity**

As the platform grows, there will be continual pressure to introduce increasingly specialised features.

Main Street will resist unnecessary complexity.

Where possible:

* Existing workflows should be extended rather than duplicated.
* Automation should replace configuration.
* Simplicity should take precedence over feature quantity.

The platform should remain approachable for first-time business owners.

---

# **10.13 Main Street Will Not Compete with Its Merchants**

Main Street exists to empower merchants—not compete with them.

The platform will not:

* Sell competing products.
* Operate competing local businesses.
* Prioritise platform revenue over merchant success.
* Insert itself unnecessarily into merchant-customer relationships.

Merchant success is platform success.

---

# **10.14 Main Street Will Not Lock Merchants In**

Merchants should retain ownership of their businesses and business information.

The platform should avoid practices that unnecessarily prevent merchants from:

* Accessing their own data.
* Exporting business information where appropriate.
* Controlling their customer relationships.
* Managing their digital identity.

Main Street should earn loyalty through value rather than dependency.

---

# **10.15 Product Discipline**

Every future product proposal should answer one question:

> **Does this strengthen Main Street's role as the Digital Presence Operating System for local businesses?**

If the answer is no, the proposal should normally be rejected.

Maintaining strategic focus is more valuable than expanding into adjacent markets without a compelling rationale.

---

# **10.16 Non-Goals Statement**

Main Street is intentionally opinionated.

It does not seek to become every type of business software.

Instead, it seeks to become the world's most trusted Digital Presence Operating System for local businesses.

This requires disciplined product management, deliberate prioritisation and the courage to decline opportunities that do not align with the platform's mission.

By defining what Main Street will not become, the platform preserves the clarity necessary to achieve what it aspires to become.
