# Marketing & Campaign Engine — Historical Product Intent

> **Section:** 25  
> **Status:** Historical product-intent evidence — non-authoritative  
> **Current governing authority:** MS-PROT-087 v1.0 composed with MS-PROT-043, MS-PROT-046, MS-PROT-053, MS-PROT-057, MS-PROT-075, MS-PROT-083, MS-PROT-086 and applicable source/provider authorities

The former Marketing & Campaign Engine PRD is retained through Git history as early product-intent evidence. It does not govern current implementation.

MS-PROT-087 now establishes the bounded Campaign purpose/revision, Audience Definition, campaign-specific Recipient Eligibility Assessment, exact approval, Automated Campaign Contract, Campaign Occurrence, suppression/deduplication and outcome-evidence semantics required for merchant marketing.

The former assumptions that CRM data automatically establishes segments, CustomerContext or prior trade establishes marketing consent, AI may select recipients or optimise delivery without registered authority, Campaign owns Publication or Notification delivery, or campaign analytics may directly claim bookings/orders/revenue generated are superseded within overlapping scope.

Main Street remains focused on simple, low-administration merchant outreach rather than enterprise CRM, customer-data-platform, funnel-builder or advertising-suite depth. Public outreach composes through MS-PROT-046, direct delivery through MS-PROT-075, replies through MS-PROT-086, protected-data use through MS-PROT-053 and applicable regulatory authority, and measures/recommendations/attribution through MS-PROT-083.

Production campaign, audience, permission/suppression, automated-trigger, performance-measure and paid-advertising decisions remain gated by `MS-PROT-087-DQ-001` through `MS-PROT-087-DQ-006`. Acceptance does not promote Marketing implementation, select a provider, authorise external advertising spend or alter the current implementation programme.
