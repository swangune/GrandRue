# **19. Announcement System**

## **19.1 Purpose**

The Announcement System enables merchants to communicate directly with customers and visitors through timely business updates.

Unlike social media platforms, Main Street does not facilitate conversations between users. Instead, it provides a structured, one-way communication channel that keeps customers informed while maintaining the merchant's professional image.

Announcements represent the "living" aspect of a business website, demonstrating that the business is active, responsive and engaged with its community.

---

# **19.2 Design Philosophy**

The Announcement System is guided by a simple principle:

> **Websites should communicate, not become social networks.**

Main Street is **not** a social media platform.

Announcements exist to inform customers, not to encourage online discussions.

Customer conversations should occur directly with the business through existing communication channels such as telephone, email, messaging or in person.

---

# **19.3 Objectives**

The Announcement System shall:

* Keep websites fresh.
* Encourage repeat visits.
* Improve customer awareness.
* Increase search engine activity.
* Reduce duplicate posting across platforms.
* Promote products and services.
* Strengthen local customer relationships.

---

# **19.4 Core Principles**

## Merchant-Only Publishing

Only authorised merchant accounts may create announcements.

Customers and visitors cannot publish content.

---

## One-Way Communication

Announcements are broadcast by the business.

Visitors cannot:

* Comment.
* Reply.
* Quote.
* React.
* Mention other users.
* Create discussion threads.

This preserves the professional nature of the business website.

---

## Shareable Content

Although visitors cannot interact socially within Main Street, they should be able to share announcements externally.

Supported sharing options include:

* Copy link.
* Share to messaging apps.
* Share to social media.
* Email.

Sharing expands the merchant's reach without introducing social networking features.

---

## Merchant Ownership

Every announcement belongs exclusively to the merchant.

Main Street does not promote announcements from one business to another.

There is no central announcement feed.

Customers only see announcements when visiting the merchant's website or through authorised syndication.

---

# **19.5 Announcement Lifecycle**

Each announcement follows the same lifecycle:

1. Draft.
2. Preview.
3. Publish.
4. Synchronise.
5. Archive.

Published announcements remain available unless intentionally removed by the merchant.

---

# **19.6 Announcement Composition**

An announcement may include:

### Required

* Announcement text.

### Optional

* Title.
* Images.
* Video.
* Product references.
* Service references.
* Booking links.
* Website links.
* Promotional banners.
* Expiry date.
* Scheduled publication date.

The interface should encourage concise, high-impact updates rather than long-form articles.

---

# **19.7 Writing Experience**

The editor should feel familiar to merchants who already use social media.

Features include:

* Plain text editor.
* Image upload.
* Video upload.
* Emoji support.
* Link insertion.
* Live preview.
* Character counter.

Formatting options should remain intentionally simple.

---

# **19.8 Announcement Feed**

The website homepage includes a dedicated **Announcements** section.

Announcements are displayed in reverse chronological order.

Each announcement card may display:

* Publication date.
* Text preview.
* Featured media.
* Business branding.
* Call-to-action buttons.

The feed should reinforce that the business is active and current.

---

# **19.9 Dedicated Announcement Pages**

Every published announcement automatically generates its own webpage.

Each page includes:

* Permanent URL.
* SEO metadata.
* Structured data.
* Open Graph metadata.
* Sharing buttons.
* Related products.
* Related services.
* Navigation back to the main website.

These pages contribute to search engine visibility while preserving a coherent website structure.

---

# **19.10 Automatic Social Media Syndication**

If the merchant has connected supported social media accounts, Main Street should automatically publish the announcement to those platforms.

The syndication engine should:

* Adapt media sizes.
* Respect character limits.
* Optimise formatting.
* Include links back to the merchant's website where appropriate.

Merchants write once.

Main Street distributes everywhere.

---

# **19.11 Scheduling**

Merchants may:

* Publish immediately.
* Schedule publication.
* Set expiry dates.
* Save drafts.

Scheduling enables merchants to plan campaigns in advance.

---

# **19.12 AI Assistance**

AI may assist merchants by:

* Improving grammar.
* Suggesting shorter wording.
* Creating promotional copy.
* Generating hashtags for supported social platforms.
* Recommending calls to action.
* Translating announcements into supported languages.

AI should never publish without merchant approval.

---

# **19.13 Search Engine Benefits**

Announcements provide continuously updated content that improves website freshness.

Each published announcement should:

* Be indexable.
* Generate structured metadata.
* Update XML sitemaps.
* Create internal links.
* Improve topical relevance.

This enables small businesses to maintain active websites without maintaining a traditional blog.

---

# **19.14 Customer Experience**

Visitors may:

* Read announcements.
* Share announcements.
* Open attached products.
* Open attached services.
* Follow booking links.
* Contact the business.

Visitors may not:

* Create accounts solely to interact with announcements.
* Comment.
* Reply.
* Like.
* Dislike.
* Rate announcements.

The focus remains on informing customers rather than creating online communities.

---

# **19.15 Moderation**

Because only merchants can publish, moderation requirements are significantly reduced.

Main Street reserves the right to remove announcements that violate:

* Applicable laws.
* Platform Terms of Service.
* Intellectual property rights.
* Prohibited content policies.

Merchants remain legally responsible for the accuracy and legality of their published announcements.

---

# **19.16 Analytics**

The platform should provide merchants with announcement performance insights, including:

* Total views.
* Unique visitors.
* Shares.
* Click-through rates.
* Product clicks.
* Booking conversions.
* Purchase conversions.

These insights help merchants understand which announcements generate business outcomes.

---

# **19.17 Announcement Retention**

Merchants may:

* Archive announcements.
* Republish archived announcements.
* Delete announcements.
* Duplicate announcements for future campaigns.

Archived announcements should remain accessible to merchants but no longer appear publicly unless republished.

---

# **19.18 Announcement Statement**

The Announcement System transforms static business websites into dynamic, regularly updated digital storefronts.

By combining one-way business communication, automatic website publishing, intelligent social media syndication and search engine optimisation, Main Street enables merchants to maintain an active online presence without becoming another social media platform.

Announcements are not conversations.

They are the digital equivalent of updating the noticeboard in a shop window—except every update is instantly reflected across the merchant's digital presence.

---
