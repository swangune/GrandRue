# **34. Privacy & Compliance**

## **34.1 Purpose**

The Privacy & Compliance framework defines how Main Street collects, processes, stores, shares and protects personal information while ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

Privacy is a core element of customer trust and merchant confidence.

Main Street shall enable merchants to operate professionally without requiring them to become experts in privacy legislation.

The platform provides privacy infrastructure, while merchants remain responsible for how they conduct their own businesses.

---

# **34.2 Design Philosophy**

The Privacy & Compliance framework is founded on one principle:

> **Collect only what is needed, protect everything that is collected.**

Every item of personal information should have a clear business purpose.

If information is unnecessary, it should not be collected.

---

# **34.3 Objectives**

The Privacy & Compliance framework shall:

* Protect personal information.
* Support merchant compliance.
* Respect customer privacy.
* Minimise data collection.
* Promote transparency.
* Support regulatory obligations.
* Enable secure data management.

---

# **34.4 Privacy Principles**

The platform shall follow these principles.

### Data Minimisation

Only information necessary to deliver the requested service should be collected.

---

### Purpose Limitation

Personal information shall only be used for legitimate business purposes.

---

### Transparency

Customers should understand:

* What information is collected.
* Why it is collected.
* How it is used.
* Who is responsible for it.

---

### Security

All personal information shall be protected using appropriate technical and organisational safeguards.

---

### Accountability

Every merchant remains responsible for how they use customer information obtained through their own business.

Main Street provides the infrastructure but does not become the operator of the merchant's business.

---

# **34.5 Applicable Regulations**

The platform should be designed to support compliance with applicable legislation, including:

* UK GDPR.
* EU GDPR.
* Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), where applicable.
* Other regional privacy regulations as the platform expands internationally.

Compliance requirements should be adaptable to future legal developments.

---

# **34.6 Data Controller Responsibilities**

Main Street and merchants have different responsibilities.

### Main Street

Acts as the platform provider and is responsible for protecting platform infrastructure and processing data necessary to provide the service.

### Merchant

Acts as the operator of their business and is responsible for:

* Customer communications.
* Marketing activities.
* Business policies.
* Legal obligations relating to their own customers.
* The accuracy of information published on their website.

This separation should be clearly communicated throughout the platform.

---

# **34.7 Personal Information**

Examples of personal information processed by the platform include:

Merchant Information

* Business owner name.
* Contact information.
* Business address.
* Payment information.
* Subscription details.

Customer Information

* Name.
* Email address.
* Telephone number.
* Booking history.
* Order history.
* Communication preferences.

The platform shall avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

---

# **34.8 Customer Consent**

Where consent is required, the platform shall support:

* Marketing consent.
* Cookie consent.
* Communication preferences.
* Future consent categories.

Consent records shall be stored securely and remain auditable.

---

# **34.9 Cookie Management**

Merchant websites shall support configurable cookie management.

Cookie categories may include:

* Essential cookies.
* Analytics cookies.
* Marketing cookies.
* Functional cookies.

Customers should be able to manage non-essential cookie preferences where required by law.

---

# **34.10 Privacy Policies**

Main Street shall assist merchants by generating an initial privacy policy based on:

* Business type.
* Services offered.
* Contact information.
* Selected platform features.

Merchants remain responsible for reviewing and ensuring the policy accurately reflects their business practices.

---

# **34.11 Terms & Conditions**

The platform may generate draft Terms & Conditions during onboarding using AI-assisted templates.

The generated documents are intended to reduce administrative effort.

Merchants remain responsible for reviewing and approving them before publication.

---

# **34.12 Customer Rights**

Where applicable, the platform shall support requests relating to:

* Access to personal information.
* Correction of inaccurate information.
* Deletion of information.
* Data portability.
* Restriction of processing.

The platform provides the technical capability for merchants to fulfil these obligations.

---

# **34.13 Data Retention**

Personal information should not be retained indefinitely.

Retention periods shall consider:

* Legal requirements.
* Business requirements.
* Operational necessity.

Expired information should be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

---

# **34.14 Data Portability**

Merchants shall be able to export their own business data.

Examples include:

* Customer records.
* Products.
* Services.
* Orders.
* Bookings.
* Announcements.
* Analytics.
* Marketing history.

This reinforces merchant ownership of their business information.

---

# **34.15 Right to Erasure**

Where legally appropriate, merchants should be able to fulfil customer requests to delete personal information.

Deletion processes should preserve records required by law while removing unnecessary personal information.

---

# **34.16 International Data Transfers**

If personal information is processed outside the merchant's jurisdiction, appropriate safeguards shall be implemented in accordance with applicable regulations.

The platform should remain flexible as infrastructure evolves internationally.

---

# **34.17 AI & Privacy**

Artificial Intelligence shall process only the information necessary to perform its intended function.

AI features shall:

* Respect customer privacy.
* Avoid unnecessary personal information processing.
* Operate transparently where appropriate.
* Allow merchants to review AI-generated outputs.

AI should assist business operations without compromising privacy.

---

# **34.18 Privacy by Design**

Every new feature introduced into Main Street shall consider privacy during design and development.

Privacy considerations include:

* Data minimisation.
* Secure defaults.
* Access controls.
* Retention policies.
* Transparency.

Privacy shall not be treated as an afterthought.

---

# **34.19 Compliance Monitoring**

The platform should support ongoing compliance through:

* Audit logs.
* Consent records.
* Administrative reporting.
* Security monitoring.
* Policy updates.

Compliance is a continuous operational responsibility rather than a one-time exercise.

---

# **34.20 Future Privacy Enhancements**

The architecture should support future capabilities including:

* Automated data retention policies.
* AI-assisted compliance recommendations.
* Enhanced consent management.
* Region-specific regulatory controls.
* Privacy impact assessments.

The framework should evolve alongside changing legal requirements.

---

# **34.21 Privacy & Compliance Statement**

The Privacy & Compliance framework enables merchants to operate responsibly while protecting the personal information entrusted to them.

By combining secure infrastructure, transparent data practices, consent management and compliance support, Main Street helps businesses meet modern privacy expectations without unnecessary administrative complexity.

Privacy is not merely a legal obligation—it is fundamental to earning and maintaining the trust of merchants and their customers.
