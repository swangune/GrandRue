# Business Operations — BO-03 Customer Communication — Historical Draft Evidence

> **Workflow ID:** BO-03
> **Module:** Business Operations
> **Status:** Historical Draft evidence — non-authoritative
> **Current governing authority:** MS-PROT-086 v1.0 composed with MS-PROT-085 and the applicable source, access and Notification authorities

The former linear workflow and its universal “conversation concluded/reopened” semantics are retained through Git history only.

MS-PROT-086 governs current Customer Communication semantics. Durable messaging may create or reuse a Conversation under an accepted contract, accept immutable Messages, dispatch outbound communication through Notification and contribute inbound Messages to Merchant Attention only through an activated Attention Contract.

A human or automated response does not itself execute an order, booking, payment, refund, cancellation or other source operation. Any business consequence requires the applicable source-owner instruction and current runtime authority.

The accepted authority does not require a merchant to return to a named Operational Workspace, use a universal conversation state machine or enable AI. Presentation and implementation remain downstream of the accepted programme and production portfolio gates.
