# Business Operations

> **Module ID:** BO-00
> **Module:** Business Operations
> **Status:** Draft
> **Owner:** Product Team

---

# 1. Purpose

Business Operations defines how merchants operate their businesses after successfully completing onboarding.

It provides the operational framework through which merchants manage customers, fulfil orders, deliver services, communicate, receive payments and administer their businesses from a unified platform.

Unlike Merchant Onboarding, which is completed once, Business Operations represents the merchant's continuous day-to-day interaction with Main Street.

---

# 2. Objectives

Business Operations aims to:

* Provide a simple and consistent operational experience.
* Minimise the effort required to run a local business.
* Present operational work in a clear and prioritised manner.
* Reduce context switching between business activities.
* Support both manual and AI-assisted workflows.
* Adapt to different commerce profiles without changing the merchant experience.

---

# 3. Philosophy

Business Operations is designed around how merchants work rather than how software is organised.

Merchants should focus on serving customers and operating their businesses without needing to understand the underlying platform structure.

Operational complexity shall remain hidden behind a simple and intuitive interface.

---

# 4. Design Principles

Business Operations follows these principles.

## Operational Simplicity

The platform shall present simple operational experiences while managing complex business processes internally.

---

## Unified Experience

Merchants shall operate their businesses through a single workspace rather than multiple disconnected applications.

---

## Attention-Driven Operations

The platform shall prioritise work requiring immediate attention before presenting historical information or analytics.

---

## AI-Assisted Operation

Artificial Intelligence shall assist merchants with repetitive, time-consuming and low-risk activities while leaving business decisions under merchant control.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

Information shall be revealed only when required.

The interface should avoid overwhelming merchants with unnecessary controls or information.

---

## Controlled Automation

Where confidence is sufficiently high, repetitive operational tasks may be automated.

Where business judgement is required, merchants shall remain in control.

---

## Evidence-Driven Evolution

The operational experience shall evolve using merchant feedback, usability testing and operational analytics rather than assumptions.

---

# 5. Operational Scope

Business Operations includes the following operational domains.

* Customer Communication
* Unified POS
* Orders
* Bookings
* Products
* Services
* Inventory
* Payments
* Customers
* Business Management
* Analytics
* Administration

Each operational domain remains independently modular while contributing to a unified merchant experience.

---

# 6. Merchant Experience

Merchants should experience Main Street as a single business operating system.

Business Operations shall minimise navigation, reduce repetitive tasks and surface work that requires immediate attention.

The merchant should spend more time serving customers and less time managing software.

---

# 7. Relationship to Other Modules

Business Operations begins after successful completion of Merchant Onboarding.

It interacts with:

* Merchant Onboarding
* Customer Journey
* Platform Services
* AI Services
* External Integrations

Business Operations remains active throughout the lifetime of the merchant account.

---

# 8. Success Criteria

Business Operations is successful when merchants can efficiently operate their businesses through a unified operational experience while maintaining simplicity, reliability and scalability.
