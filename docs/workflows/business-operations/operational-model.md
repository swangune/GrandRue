# Business Operations — Operational Model

> **Document ID:** BO-00.1
> **Module:** Business Operations
> **Status:** Draft
> **Owner:** Product Team

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the operational model used throughout the Main Street platform.

The operational model describes how merchants interact with the platform while running their businesses.

It serves as the foundation for all Business Operations workflows.

---

# 2. Philosophy

Business Operations is organised around business activities rather than software modules.

Merchants should think about operating their businesses, not navigating software.

Main Street shall present a unified operational experience while managing underlying complexity internally.

---

# 3. Operational Workspace

The Operational Workspace is the merchant's primary working environment.

It provides immediate visibility of:

* Work requiring attention.
* Active customer interactions.
* Current business activity.
* Quick operational actions.
* Business health.

The Operational Workspace is not a reporting dashboard.

Its primary purpose is to help merchants perform work.

---

# 4. Operational Navigation

Business Operations consists of two navigation layers.

## Operational Dock

The Operational Dock provides immediate access to the merchant's most frequently used operational functions.

The dock is permanently visible.

It shall contain no more than five primary operational entries.

The final categories shall be determined through user-centred design and validated through merchant feedback after platform launch.

---

## Administration Menu

The Administration Menu is accessed through a collapsible navigation panel.

It contains configuration and administrative functions that are not part of daily operations.

Examples include:

* Business Profile
* Website
* Staff
* Integrations
* Subscription
* Security
* Settings

---

# 5. Operational Flow

Business Operations revolves around continuous operational activities.

Typical activities include:

* Serving customers.
* Managing transactions.
* Responding to enquiries.
* Maintaining business information.
* Monitoring business performance.

These activities may occur in any order throughout the business day.

Business Operations therefore follows a cyclical rather than linear model.

---

# 6. Attention Management

The platform shall continuously identify operational work requiring merchant attention.

Examples include:

* Customer conversations awaiting replies.
* New orders.
* Booking requests.
* Inventory alerts.
* Payment issues.
* Platform notifications.

The merchant should never need to search for urgent work.

---

# 7. AI Assistance

Artificial Intelligence operates alongside the merchant throughout Business Operations.

AI may:

* Recommend actions.
* Draft responses.
* Summarise conversations.
* Prioritise work.
* Detect operational risks.
* Automate routine tasks.

Final business decisions remain under merchant control unless explicit automation has been configured.

---

# 8. Operational Context

Merchants should remain within a single operational context whenever possible.

The platform shall minimise unnecessary navigation between unrelated screens.

Relevant information and actions should be presented together to reduce cognitive load.

---

# 9. Future Evolution

The operational experience is intentionally designed to evolve.

Usage analytics, merchant feedback and usability research shall guide future interface improvements while preserving operational simplicity.
