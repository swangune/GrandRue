# Business Operations — BO-01 Operational Workspace

> **Workflow ID:** BO-01
> **Module:** Business Operations
> **Status:** Draft
> **Owner:** Product Team

---

# 1. Purpose

The Operational Workspace is the primary working environment for merchants after onboarding.

It provides a unified operational view of the business, enabling merchants to monitor activity, respond to customer needs and initiate business operations from a single location.

The Operational Workspace acts as the operational home of the Main Street platform.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Operational Workspace aims to:

* Present work requiring immediate attention.
* Provide rapid access to common business operations.
* Display current business status.
* Reduce unnecessary navigation.
* Support AI-assisted decision making.
* Provide a consistent starting point for all operational workflows.

---

# 3. Philosophy

The Operational Workspace is not a reporting dashboard.

It is an operational control centre.

Its purpose is to help merchants complete work rather than simply display information.

Information is prioritised according to operational importance rather than functional ownership.

---

# 4. Actors

## Primary Actor

* Merchant

## Supporting Actors

* Main Street Platform
* AI Operational Assistant

---

# 5. Entry Points

Merchants may enter the Operational Workspace from:

* Merchant sign-in.
* Completion of onboarding.
* Completion of any operational workflow.
* Selecting the Home action from the Operational Dock.
* Returning from the Administration Menu.

The Operational Workspace remains continuously available throughout business operations.

---

# 6. Operational Responsibilities

The Operational Workspace shall:

* Present operational work requiring attention.
* Surface urgent business events.
* Provide quick operational actions.
* Display business health.
* Provide access to operational workflows.
* Present AI recommendations.
* Maintain awareness of ongoing business activity.

The Operational Workspace shall not contain business logic.

Business logic remains within the relevant operational workflows and platform services.

---

# 7. Operational Sections

The Operational Workspace consists of the following logical sections.

## Work Queue

Displays operational activities requiring merchant attention.

Examples include:

* Customer conversations.
* Pending bookings.
* New orders.
* Payment exceptions.
* Inventory alerts.

---

## Quick Actions

Provides rapid access to frequently performed operations.

The available actions may vary according to the merchant's commerce profile.

---

## Business Health

Displays the operational status of key platform services.

Examples include:

* Website availability.
* Payment processing.
* Google Business Profile synchronisation.
* AI Operational Assistant.
* Notification services.

---

## AI Recommendations

Presents recommendations generated by AI.

Examples include:

* Suggested replies.
* Stock replenishment.
* Appointment optimisation.
* Customer follow-ups.
* Business insights.

---

# 8. Operational Dock

The Operational Dock provides immediate access to the platform's primary operational capabilities.

The dock:

* remains permanently visible;
* contains a maximum of five operational entries;
* prioritises business activities over software modules;
* shall remain consistent across the platform.

The exact operational categories shall be defined through user-centred design and refined using production usage data.

---

# 9. Administration Menu

Administrative functions are accessed through a collapsible navigation menu.

These functions support business configuration rather than daily operations.

Examples include:

* Business Profile.
* Website Management.
* Staff Management.
* Integrations.
* Subscription.
* Security.
* Settings.

---

# 10. AI Responsibilities

The AI Operational Assistant may:

* Prioritise operational work.
* Recommend actions.
* Draft responses.
* Highlight business risks.
* Summarise operational activity.

The AI shall not perform irreversible business actions without merchant approval unless explicit automation has been configured.

---

# 11. Success Criteria

The Operational Workspace is successful when the merchant can immediately understand:

* what requires attention;
* the current state of the business;
* recommended actions;
* where to begin work.

Every operational workflow should naturally begin and end at the Operational Workspace, ensuring merchants maintain awareness of their business without unnecessary navigation.
