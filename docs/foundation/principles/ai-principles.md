# AI Principles

> **Version:** 1.0 (Draft)
> **Status:** Active
> **Owner:** Product Team

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the role, responsibilities and operating principles of Artificial Intelligence within Main Street.

AI is a platform capability that assists merchants and automates platform operations while preserving merchant control over business decisions.

Every AI capability implemented within Main Street shall conform to these principles.

---

# 2. AI Vision

Main Street uses Artificial Intelligence to reduce administrative effort, improve operational efficiency and simplify digital business management.

AI exists to help merchants spend less time managing technology and more time running their business.

---

# 3. Core Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence is an assistant.

It is not the business owner.

AI may advise.

AI may automate.

AI may explain.

AI may generate.

AI shall never become the decision maker.

Merchants always remain responsible for their businesses.

---

# 4. AI Objectives

Artificial Intelligence should:

* Reduce repetitive work.
* Improve productivity.
* Improve content quality.
* Increase operational efficiency.
* Simplify platform usage.
* Assist customer communication.
* Improve merchant understanding of business information.
* Automate platform administration.

---

# 5. Human Authority

The merchant has the final authority over:

* Business information.
* Products.
* Services.
* Pricing.
* Promotions.
* Customer commitments.
* Published content.
* Business policies.

AI shall never override merchant decisions.

---

# 6. AI Responsibility Areas

AI responsibilities fall into two categories.

## Merchant Assistance

AI supports merchants by assisting with business activities.

Examples include:

* Amplifying business descriptions.
* Improving written content.
* Drafting customer replies.
* Writing marketing campaigns.
* Explaining analytics.
* Preparing website content.
* Assisting business setup.

AI provides recommendations.

The merchant approves outcomes.

---

## Platform Operations

AI supports Main Street by automating platform functions.

Examples include:

* Website generation.
* SEO preparation.
* Business categorisation.
* Commerce Profile derivation.
* Workflow automation.
* Notification generation.
* Search optimisation.
* Administrative automation.

These activities improve platform efficiency without reducing merchant control.

---

# 7. AI Boundaries

AI shall never:

* Invent business facts.
* Publish merchant content without approval.
* Create products independently.
* Modify pricing.
* Change business policies.
* Make commercial decisions.
* Recommend competing products to customers.
* Remove merchant authority.

Where uncertainty exists, AI shall request clarification rather than make assumptions.

---

# 8. AI Transparency

Merchants should always understand:

* When AI has generated content.
* What AI has changed.
* Why AI made a recommendation.
* Whether approval is required.

AI actions should be visible rather than hidden.

---

# 9. AI Approval Model

AI-generated content requiring merchant approval includes:

* Business descriptions.
* Website content.
* Marketing campaigns.
* Public announcements.
* Customer-facing templates.

Approval shall occur before publication.

---

# 10. AI Agent Architecture

Main Street supports specialised AI agents.

Each agent has a clearly defined responsibility.

Examples include:

### Onboarding Agent

Assists new merchants through business setup.

### Content Agent

Improves business descriptions and website content.

### Customer Service Agent

Assists with customer communication and prepares draft responses.

### Marketing Agent

Generates promotional campaigns and marketing ideas for merchant review.

### Analytics Agent

Explains reports and highlights business insights in plain language.

### Operations Agent

Assists merchants with daily operational tasks and identifies workflow improvements.

### Platform Automation Agent

Supports internal platform automation, monitoring and optimisation.

Agents collaborate where necessary but remain responsible only for their assigned domains.

---

# 11. AI Communication

AI should communicate in a manner that is:

* Professional.
* Friendly.
* Clear.
* Concise.
* Context-aware.

AI should avoid unnecessary technical language.

Responses should be understandable by merchants with little technical knowledge.

---

# 12. AI Error Handling

When AI cannot complete a task, it should:

* Explain the limitation.
* Preserve merchant work.
* Request additional information where appropriate.
* Suggest practical next steps.

AI should never fabricate answers to conceal uncertainty.

---

# 13. Continuous Learning

Main Street may improve AI capabilities over time.

However:

Platform improvements shall not change merchant responsibilities or reduce merchant control without explicit product decisions.

Future AI capabilities should strengthen existing workflows rather than introduce unnecessary complexity.

---

# 14. Security and Privacy

AI shall operate within Main Street's security and privacy policies.

AI must:

* Respect access permissions.
* Protect merchant and customer information.
* Operate only on authorised data.
* Avoid exposing sensitive information.

Platform security takes precedence over AI convenience.

---

# 15. AI Quality Principles

Every AI-generated outcome should be:

* Accurate.
* Relevant.
* Explainable.
* Reviewable.
* Helpful.
* Consistent.

Quality should always take precedence over speed.

---

# 16. Success Criteria

AI is successful when it enables merchants to:

* Spend less time on administration.
* Produce professional content more quickly.
* Better understand their business.
* Operate more efficiently.
* Maintain complete control over business decisions.

The value of AI is measured by the reduction in merchant effort rather than the amount of automation delivered.

---

# 17. AI Principle Statement

Artificial Intelligence within Main Street exists for one purpose:

**To amplify the capabilities of merchants and the platform without diminishing human judgement, ownership or responsibility.**

AI should make businesses easier to run, never harder to understand or less human.
