# Main Street Platform

> **Version:** 1.0 (Draft)
> **Status:** Active
> **Owner:** Product Team

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Main Street Platform as a system actor.

The platform provides the technology, infrastructure and orchestration required for merchants to operate their businesses digitally.

It coordinates workflows, business services, integrations and platform capabilities while remaining largely invisible to customers.

All platform-related workflows reference this document.

---

# 2. Definition

The Main Street Platform is the digital commerce infrastructure that powers merchant businesses.

It is responsible for building, operating, maintaining and securing the merchant's digital ecosystem.

The platform is not the merchant's business.

It is the technology partner that enables the business to operate efficiently.

---

# 3. Mission

The mission of the platform is to remove technological complexity from running a business.

Merchants should focus on customers, products and services while the platform manages the underlying technology.

---

# 4. Platform Responsibilities

The platform is responsible for:

* Merchant account management.
* Authentication and authorisation.
* Workflow orchestration.
* Website generation.
* Unified POS infrastructure.
* Business data management.
* Security.
* Performance.
* Reliability.
* Monitoring.
* AI services.
* Notifications.
* External integrations.
* Analytics infrastructure.
* Search and SEO infrastructure.

The platform provides these capabilities without becoming part of the merchant-customer relationship.

---

# 5. Platform Principles

The platform operates according to the following principles:

* Technology should remain largely invisible.
* Merchant branding always takes priority.
* Business decisions belong to merchants.
* Automation should reduce effort without reducing control.
* Security is built into every service.
* Simplicity is preferred over unnecessary complexity.

---

# 6. Platform Capabilities

The platform provides the following core capabilities.

## Business Infrastructure

Supports merchant onboarding, business configuration and operational readiness.

---

## Website Infrastructure

Generates, hosts and maintains merchant websites.

---

## Commerce Infrastructure

Supports product sales, service bookings and hybrid transactions through the Unified POS.

---

## Communication Infrastructure

Supports customer notifications, messaging and reminders.

---

## AI Infrastructure

Provides intelligent assistance to merchants and staff through the AI Assistant while respecting merchant authority.

---

## Integration Infrastructure

Connects merchants with approved third-party services including payment providers, mapping services, Google Business Profile and future platform integrations.

---

## Operational Infrastructure

Supports analytics, reporting, monitoring, auditing and internal automation.

---

# 7. Workflow Orchestration

The platform coordinates all approved business workflows.

Examples include:

* Merchant onboarding.
* Website publishing.
* Customer ordering.
* Appointment scheduling.
* Hybrid transactions.
* Payment processing.
* Notification delivery.

Business rules remain within their respective modules.

The platform coordinates execution rather than making business decisions.

---

# 8. Relationship with Merchants

The platform provides merchants with the tools required to operate digitally.

It assists merchants but never assumes ownership of their business.

Merchants remain responsible for:

* Commercial decisions.
* Customer relationships.
* Business policies.
* Products and services.

---

# 9. Relationship with Customers

Customers do not establish relationships with the Main Street Platform.

Customers interact directly with merchant businesses.

The platform operates in the background as invisible infrastructure supporting the merchant's digital presence.

Where acknowledgement is appropriate, it should be limited to discreet attribution such as **"Powered by Main Street."**

---

# 10. Relationship with AI

The AI Assistant is a capability provided by the platform.

The platform governs:

* AI security.
* AI permissions.
* AI availability.
* AI integration with workflows.

The platform remains responsible for ensuring AI operates within approved business rules.

---

# 11. Relationship with External Services

The platform manages communication with approved third-party systems.

Examples include:

* Google Business Profile.
* Payment providers.
* Email providers.
* SMS providers.
* Mapping services.
* Future delivery partners.

External services are isolated behind platform interfaces to minimise coupling and simplify future changes.

---

# 12. Security Responsibilities

The platform is responsible for:

* Authentication.
* Authorisation.
* Encryption.
* Audit logging.
* Data protection.
* Availability.
* Backup and recovery.
* Secure integrations.

Security applies consistently across every platform capability.

---

# 13. Reliability Responsibilities

The platform should provide:

* High availability.
* Operational resilience.
* Fault tolerance.
* Graceful degradation.
* Background recovery.
* Continuous monitoring.

Merchants should experience dependable service even when individual components encounter temporary failures.

---

# 14. Design Principles

The platform should be:

* Reliable.
* Secure.
* Scalable.
* Modular.
* Observable.
* Maintainable.
* Extensible.
* Merchant-focused.

Every capability should simplify business operations without exposing unnecessary technical complexity.

---

# 15. Success Criteria

The platform is successful when:

* Merchants can operate confidently.
* Customers experience professional digital interactions.
* Business workflows remain unified.
* Technology remains largely invisible.
* AI improves productivity without reducing merchant control.
* New capabilities can be introduced without disrupting existing businesses.

---

# 16. Platform Statement

The Main Street Platform is the invisible technology partner behind every merchant.

It designs, builds, operates and maintains digital commerce infrastructure so merchants can focus on serving customers and growing their businesses.

The platform succeeds when merchants are recognised for excellent customer experiences while the technology quietly enables those experiences behind the scenes.
