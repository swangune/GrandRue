# Staff

> **Version:** 1.0 (Draft)
> **Status:** Active
> **Owner:** Product Team

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Staff actor within Main Street.

Staff members assist merchants in operating a business through delegated responsibilities and permissions.

They do not own the business.

All staff-related workflows reference this document.

---

# 2. Definition

A Staff Member is an individual authorised by a merchant to perform specific operational or administrative activities on behalf of a business.

Staff permissions are granted by the merchant and may be modified or revoked at any time.

---

# 3. Primary Goal

The purpose of staff access is to enable businesses to delegate work safely while maintaining merchant ownership and accountability.

---

# 4. Ownership

Staff members never own the business.

Only the Merchant has ownership authority.

Business ownership cannot be transferred through ordinary staff permissions.

---

# 5. Staff Categories

Main Street supports flexible staff roles.

Examples include:

* Manager
* Receptionist
* Cashier
* Sales Assistant
* Barber
* Stylist
* Mechanic
* Consultant
* Administrator
* Delivery Staff

These are business roles rather than fixed platform roles.

Merchants may assign one or more permission sets appropriate to each staff member.

---

# 6. Permission Model

Permissions are capability-based rather than job-title based.

Examples include:

* Manage Orders
* Manage Schedules
* Process Payments
* View Customers
* Manage Inventory
* Manage Products
* Manage Services
* Respond to Messages
* Publish Website Changes
* View Reports
* Manage Staff
* Manage Settings

Each permission is granted independently.

---

# 7. Principle of Least Privilege

Staff members should receive only the permissions required to perform their responsibilities.

Unnecessary access should not be granted.

This reduces operational risk and improves security.

---

# 8. Operational Activities

Depending on permissions, staff may:

* Create orders.
* Manage schedules.
* Record walk-in customers.
* Record telephone bookings.
* Process hybrid transactions.
* Update inventory.
* Receive customer payments.
* Respond to customer enquiries.

Operational activities should be fast and efficient.

---

# 9. Administrative Activities

Authorised staff may perform administrative tasks including:

* Managing products.
* Managing services.
* Updating business information.
* Managing website content.
* Viewing reports.
* Managing customer records.

Sensitive administrative capabilities should remain under merchant control unless explicitly delegated.

---

# 10. Relationship with Customers

Staff members represent the merchant during customer interactions.

Customers should experience consistent service regardless of which authorised staff member assists them.

---

# 11. Relationship with AI

AI may assist staff in the same manner as merchants.

Examples include:

* Drafting replies.
* Improving written content.
* Explaining operational information.
* Assisting workflow completion.

AI should never exceed the permissions granted to the staff member.

---

# 12. Authentication

Every staff member should have an individual account.

Shared accounts are not permitted.

Individual authentication supports:

* Security.
* Accountability.
* Audit logging.
* Activity history.

---

# 13. Auditability

All significant staff actions should be recorded.

Examples include:

* Order creation.
* Appointment changes.
* Inventory adjustments.
* Refunds.
* Payment processing.
* Product updates.

Audit records should identify the individual staff member responsible for each action.

---

# 14. Merchant Authority

The merchant retains ultimate authority over:

* Staff invitations.
* Permission assignment.
* Permission removal.
* Staff suspension.
* Staff deletion.

No staff member may elevate their own permissions.

---

# 15. Design Principles

Staff management should be:

* Secure.
* Simple.
* Transparent.
* Auditable.
* Mobile-friendly.

Permission management should remain understandable for non-technical business owners.

---

# 16. Staff Statement

Staff members extend the operational capability of a business through delegated authority.

Main Street enables safe delegation while ensuring that ownership, accountability and final business control always remain with the merchant.
