# Merchant

> **Version:** 1.0 (Draft)
> **Status:** Active
> **Owner:** Product Team

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Merchant actor within Main Street.

It specifies the merchant's responsibilities, capabilities, permissions and interactions with the platform.

All merchant-related workflows reference this document.

---

# 2. Definition

A Merchant is an individual or organisation authorised to operate a business on the Main Street platform.

The merchant owns the business and remains responsible for all commercial decisions, customer relationships and operational activities.

Main Street provides the technology that enables the merchant to operate efficiently.

---

# 3. Primary Goal

The merchant's goal is to successfully operate and grow their business while spending as little time as possible managing technology.

---

# 4. Merchant Responsibilities

The merchant remains responsible for:

* Business information.
* Products.
* Services.
* Pricing.
* Business policies.
* Customer relationships.
* Legal compliance.
* Staff management.
* Business reputation.

Main Street assists with these activities but does not assume responsibility for them.

---

# 5. Merchant Authority

The merchant has final authority over:

* Publishing business information.
* Product catalogues.
* Service catalogues.
* Prices.
* Discounts.
* Promotions.
* Website content.
* Customer communications.
* Business settings.

No AI capability or automated process may override these decisions without explicit merchant approval.

---

# 6. Merchant Categories

A merchant operates exactly one business.

Each business is automatically assigned one Commerce Profile:

* Product
* Service
* Hybrid

The Commerce Profile is derived by the Business Template Engine based on business characteristics.

Merchants do not manually select their Commerce Profile.

---

# 7. Merchant Activities

Merchant activities belong to two categories.

## Operations

Daily activities required to serve customers.

Examples include:

* Managing orders.
* Managing schedules.
* Managing hybrid transactions.
* Processing payments.
* Updating inventory.
* Responding to customers.

Operations prioritise speed and efficiency.

---

## Administration

Activities related to configuring and improving the business.

Examples include:

* Updating the business profile.
* Managing products.
* Managing services.
* Reviewing analytics.
* Configuring settings.
* Managing subscriptions.
* Managing staff.

Administration prioritises clarity and informed decision-making.

---

# 8. Merchant Permissions

The merchant may:

* Create business content.
* Edit business information.
* Publish website changes.
* Manage staff.
* Configure integrations.
* Manage operational workflows.
* Approve AI-generated content.
* Access reports and analytics.

Permissions may later be delegated to authorised staff members.

---

# 9. Merchant Relationship with AI

Artificial Intelligence acts as the merchant's business assistant.

AI may:

* Improve writing.
* Draft communications.
* Explain reports.
* Generate website content.
* Assist onboarding.
* Recommend improvements.

AI shall never:

* Publish content without approval.
* Change pricing.
* Create products independently.
* Make commercial decisions.
* Replace merchant authority.

The merchant remains accountable for every business outcome.

---

# 10. Merchant Relationship with Main Street

Main Street provides:

* Infrastructure.
* Website hosting.
* Automation.
* Security.
* AI assistance.
* Business tools.
* Platform integrations.

The merchant provides:

* Business expertise.
* Customer service.
* Commercial decisions.
* Products and services.
* Operational management.

This partnership allows merchants to focus on running their business rather than managing technology.

---

# 11. Merchant Onboarding

Every merchant begins their journey through the Merchant Onboarding workflow.

During onboarding, Main Street:

* Creates the merchant account.
* Assists business setup.
* Determines the Commerce Profile.
* Generates the initial website.
* Integrates external services where appropriate.
* Prepares the business for operation.

Completion of onboarding grants access to the Merchant Dashboard.

---

# 12. Merchant Success

A merchant is considered operational when:

* Business setup is complete.
* Commerce Profile has been assigned.
* Website has been published.
* Required verification has been completed.
* The Unified POS is available.
* The business is ready to serve customers.

---

# 13. Merchant Design Principles

Every merchant interaction should:

* Reduce effort.
* Minimise repetitive work.
* Preserve merchant control.
* Support mobile-first usage.
* Provide clear feedback.
* Be understandable without technical knowledge.

The platform should adapt to the merchant rather than expecting the merchant to adapt to the platform.

---

# 14. Merchant Statement

The Merchant is the central actor within Main Street.

Every workflow, interface, automation and AI capability should help merchants spend less time managing technology and more time serving customers and growing their business.
